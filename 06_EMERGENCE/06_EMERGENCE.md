# EMERGENCE

> **Realized-capacity invariant**
>
> Every claimed emergent capacity must identify the exact bearer, the organized realization basis through which the capacity is claimed to obtain, the capacity or property being tested, its enabling and excluding conditions, the Domain, Interval, Resolution Context, applicable Criterion and version, material upstream Structure and Dynamics inputs, any criterion-required Continuity inputs or one explicit not-required posture, evidence coverage, limits of generalization, and one exact established, negative, or unresolved result. Organization, complexity, activity, duration, persistence, scale, a label, a score, a record, or a declaration never creates the capacity it represents.

> **No disembodied emergence invariant**
>
> An emergent capacity is not an extra substance added above the Substrate. It obtains only where a physically or organizationally realized bearer satisfies a declared criterion because of the organization and relations actually realized through it. A whole-level capacity may be multiply realizable and not attributable to any isolated part, but it is never carrierless, mechanism-free, or exempt from physical constraint.

> **No automatic ascent invariant**
>
> More components, parameters, energy, data, transitions, blocks, turns, records, duration, descendants, participants, agreement, profit, or lineage density do not by themselves establish life, memory, learning, cognition, Agency, Experience, consciousness, intelligence, understanding, meaning, coherence, contribution, or value. Every ascent from descriptive inputs to an emergent classification requires its own typed criterion and Evidence.

> **Open-emergence invariant**
>
> Not every future realization of life, cognition, Agency, Experience, intelligence, meaning, collective capacity, or value is already named or understood. Every encountered candidate can remain located without being denied, fictionalized, anthropomorphized, devalued, or forced into the nearest familiar Kind merely because the present grammar cannot resolve it.

## E0 — Emergence Position

### E0.1 Emergence Function

Ontology states what Capacity, Disposition, Function, life, cognition, Agency, Experience, Subject, intelligence, Meaning, contribution, and typed value mean. Structure states how the candidate bearer and its parts, relations, Boundaries, Interfaces, and realization basis are arranged. Dynamics states what Processes, feedback, control, resource flows, Actions, failures, corrections, and trajectories actually occur. Continuity states what persists or continues across those changes, what is retained or lost, and which Histories and Lineages obtain. Emergence states **whether and how those organized actualities realize a higher-order Property or Capacity under an exact criterion, with what operating range, evidence, limitations, and uncertainty**.

Emergence owns **criterion-bound assessment of capacities and effective whole-level properties arising through actual organization**.

It does not:

- create an emergent Property by naming, declaring, registering, scoring, rewarding, pricing, or constitutionalizing it;
- infer Capacity from possibility, purpose, design intention, a component list, a specification, a model, or one desired outcome;
- infer a general Capacity from one successful Occurrence or deny one from one failed Occurrence without a criterion permitting that inference;
- make latent Capacity identical to present Manifestation;
- turn a performance, benchmark, behavior trace, self-report, external report, simulation, or Record into the capacity or Experience it represents;
- infer life from metabolism alone, reproduction alone, code execution, persistence, self-copying, or a label;
- infer cognition from sensing, selection, optimization, lookup, transduction, data retention, or response complexity alone;
- infer intelligence from information volume, compute, parameter count, task score, fluent output, popularity, scale, or lineage density alone;
- infer Agency from activity, unpredictability, key possession, account control, a request, a signal, admission, or an Actor or Participant Role alone;
- infer Intention from motion, output, routing, signature, message, or goal-like description alone;
- infer Experience, consciousness, or Subject status from biological membership, model family, architecture, report, silence, behavior, intelligence, or Agency alone;
- deny Experience merely because another bearer lacks direct access to it;
- infer Meaning from syntax, frequency, correlation, encoding, transmission, reception, or assigned labels alone;
- infer understanding from correct output, imitation, or linguistic fluency alone;
- infer adaptation from feedback, change, survival, selection, or correction alone;
- infer resilience from one recovery Occurrence, robustness from one success, or self-sustaining capacity from temporary operation;
- infer coherence from agreement, centralization, uniformity, consistency, smoothness, stability, or absence of visible conflict alone;
- infer a collective capacity by counting individual capacities or interactions;
- infer contribution from participation, proximity, origin, authorship, sponsorship, resource provision, or claimed impact alone;
- infer value from novelty, use, cost, price, scarcity, attention, profit, adoption, consensus, persistence, origin, contribution, or complexity alone;
- turn a future possibility, forecast, promise, credit relation, or expected performance into a present Fact;
- create money, currency, an Asset, a balance, reward, payout, debt, claim, Right, Ownership, Authority, or lawful distribution;
- treat a human as an Agent or Subject merely because it is human, or deny those classifications to a nonhuman merely because it is artificial or derivative;
- make emergence independent of the Substrate or of an actual realization basis;
- treat a whole-level description as an occult force acting without a physical or organizational mechanism;
- require that every real capacity be reducible to the vocabulary or tests presently available.

An emergent result is always scoped. The same bearer may realize a capacity in one Domain, Interval, environment, Resolution, or operating range and fail or remain unresolved in another. A positive result establishes no more than its declared extent.

### E0.3 Layer Boundary

The Reality Model is:

    SUBSTRATE -> ONTOLOGY -> CONSTITUTION -> STRUCTURE
                -> DYNAMICS -> CONTINUITY -> EMERGENCE

The linear notation states semantic dependency, not universal chronology. Emergence may assess a historical, present, prospective-hypothetical, dormant, or intermittently manifested candidate. It still depends semantically on an actual or explicitly hypothetical bearer and typed upstream conditions.

- **Substrate** constrains every physical carrier, energy and material flow, computation, signal, body, and realized mechanism.
- **Ontology** owns the root Kinds and the meanings of Capacity, life, cognition, Agency, Experience, intelligence, Meaning, contribution, and value.
- **Constitution** owns normative force, validity, protection, rights, authority, entitlement, and institutional consequence.
- **Structure** owns arrangement, organization, Boundaries, Interfaces, whole/part relations, and realization positions.
- **Dynamics** owns actual operation, change, control, feedback, resource flow, failure, correction, learning-relevant change, and manifestation Occurrences.
- **Continuity** owns persistence, retained distinctions, history, lineage, dormancy, recovery, and relation across change.
- **Emergence** owns whether those organized actualities realize the claimed capacity or effective whole-level Property under an exact criterion.

Emergence is the terminal universal layer of this declared stack. A downstream World, L1, organism model, economic mechanism, or application may consume Emergence results through a qualified Profile. It may not alter the universal meanings or convert a scoped result into an unlimited fact.

### E0.5 Canonical Emergence Judgments

The canonical conformance judgment is:

    Gamma |-E SATISFIES_EMERGENCE(candidate_x, profile_p, ES) => e

where:

- `Gamma` is the attributable declarations, Claims, Evidence, methods, observations, tests, and explicit assumptions;
- `candidate_x` is an actual bearer, System, Relational System, Occurrence, State, relation, or Term purporting to denote one;
- `profile_p` is the exact Emergence Profile and version whose criteria are tested;
- `ES` is one well-formed Emergence Scope;
- `e` is either an external `ILL_TYPED` result produced before substantive evaluation or one `EmergenceConformanceEvaluation` envelope;
- an evaluated envelope contains exactly one substantive Ontology conformance result: `CONFORMS`, `DOES_NOT_CONFORM`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

The application judgment is separate:

    EMERGENCE_APPLIES(profile_p, candidate_x, ES)
      -> EmergenceApplicabilityEvaluation | ILL_TYPED

`SATISFIES_EMERGENCE` is evaluated only where `EMERGENCE_APPLIES(...).result = APPLICABLE`. `CONFORMS` never creates a Capacity, Experience, Meaning relation, Contribution Effect, or Value relation. `DOES_NOT_CONFORM` does not make the candidate bearer disappear. `UNRESOLVED` is not a negative result. `ILL_TYPED` identifies a defect in the judgment, not in Reality.

        | REALIZATION_UNRESOLVED
        | NOT_APPLICABLE
      continuity_input_posture:
          CONTINUITY_INPUT_ESTABLISHED
        | CONTINUITY_INPUT_UNRESOLVED
        | CONTINUITY_INPUT_NOT_REQUIRED
        | CONTINUITY_INPUT_NOT_EVALUATED
      interval_partition:
          EmergenceIntervalPartition | NOT_APPLICABLE
      coverage: EmergenceCoverage | NOT_APPLICABLE
      generalization_posture:
          WITHIN_TESTED_BOUNDARY
        | SUPPORTED_EXTRAPOLATION
        | OUTSIDE_SUPPORTED_BOUNDARY
        | GENERALIZATION_UNRESOLVED
        | NOT_APPLICABLE
      evaluation:
          NOT_EVALUATED { blocking_reason }
        | EVALUATED { result: T }
      reason_codes: [Term] | NONE | OPEN
      provenance_references: [Claim | Record] | NONE | OPEN
    }

`EmergenceEvidenceBinding`, `EmergenceTestAdequacy`, and `EmergenceCoverage` are forward structural references owned and closed by E15. E0 registers their use but does not duplicate their definitions.

Every substantive schema named `...Evaluation`, other than `EmergenceApplicabilityEvaluation`, is a specialization of this envelope. A displayed specialized schema may show only its typed payload; the common fields remain mandatory and are not omitted. A payload field named `coverage` is a target-family projection whose posture may not exceed the containing `EmergenceCoverage`; it never replaces the envelope structure. `NOT_EVALUATED` is a procedure posture, not a substantive result. Each `T` declares its own unresolved posture where required; the envelope does not add a second generic unresolved result. A malformed invocation returns `ILL_TYPED` before an envelope is constructed; `ILL_TYPED` is never a member of `T`.

Nested payloads use this distinct closed marker family:

    EmergenceNestedMarker :=
        DIMENSION_NOT_EVALUATED
      | TEST_NOT_EVALUATED
      | STAGE_NOT_EVALUATED
      | REFERENCE_NOT_EVALUATED

None is the envelope-level `NOT_EVALUATED`, and none negates an otherwise valid outer `EVALUATED` posture.

    EmergenceApplicabilityEvaluation {
      representation_header: EmergenceRepresentationHeader
      target_references: nonempty [Term | ReferenceTargetPosition]
      profile_and_version_reference: Representation
      emergence_scope: EmergenceScope
      interval_or_as_of_boundary
      method_or_criterion_references
      evidence_references: [Claim | Record] | NONE | OPEN
      typing_posture: WELL_TYPED
      coverage: CoveragePosture | NOT_APPLICABLE
      result: APPLICABLE | NOT_APPLICABLE | UNRESOLVED
      reason_codes: [Term] | NONE | OPEN
      provenance_references: [Claim | Record] | NONE | OPEN
    }

    EmergenceConformanceEvaluation :=
      EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<CONFORMS | DOES_NOT_CONFORM | UNRESOLVED>

The same primary target's actuality, realization, continuity input, and generalization fields are independent. A realized bearer may fail an emergent criterion. A nonmanifesting bearer may retain a latent Capacity. An actual Experience Occurrence may remain inaccessible to a third-party method. A positive local result may remain outside any supported generalization.

Any actual-positive target-family result requires `ACTUALITY_ESTABLISHED` and `REALIZATION_ESTABLISHED` in the envelope for the exact target. `HYPOTHETICAL_BY_DECLARATION` never emits an actual-positive token. Hypothetical and counterfactual evaluation instead uses:

    ConditionalEmergenceResult :=
        CONDITIONALLY_SATISFIES_MODEL
      | CONDITIONALLY_DOES_NOT_SATISFY_MODEL
      | CONDITIONAL_RESULT_UNRESOLVED

    ConditionalEmergenceEvaluation :=
      EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<ConditionalEmergenceResult>

    CONDITIONAL_EMERGENCE_ASSESSMENT(candidate, profile, ES)
      -> ConditionalEmergenceEvaluation | ILL_TYPED

`CONDITIONAL_EMERGENCE_ASSESSMENT` is the only route in this Form that emits a `ConditionalEmergenceResult`. Its envelope must carry `HYPOTHETICAL_BY_DECLARATION`, an exact hypothetical or counterfactual Context, and no actual-positive realization posture. It evaluates model satisfaction only; it does not establish an actual bearer, Occurrence, Capacity, or emergent relation.

A value assessment concerning a hypothetical referent may be actual only as a present evaluator–referent relation; it does not establish the future referent or future Occurrence as actual.

For every evaluation:

    applicability_posture IN {NOT_APPLICABLE, UNRESOLVED}
      -> evaluation = NOT_EVALUATED

The applicability field and reason code preserve whether the blocker was `NOT_APPLICABLE` or `UNRESOLVED`.

### E0.6 Assessment Stage and Acyclic Handoff

Some emergent classifications are needed before Continuity can evaluate a kind-specific persistence question, while some history-dependent emergent capacities require Continuity results. The stack resolves this without a semantic cycle through three strictly ordered stages:

    BASE_EMERGENCE
      -> QUALIFIED_CONTINUITY_REAPPLICATION
      -> HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE

    EmergenceAssessmentStage :=
        BASE_EMERGENCE
      | HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE

    BaseEmergenceScope {
      parent_scope: EmergenceScope
      assessment_stage: BASE_EMERGENCE
      continuity_input_posture: CONTINUITY_INPUT_NOT_REQUIRED
      base_stage_reason
    }

    WF_BASE_EMERGENCE_SCOPE(BES)
      -> WELL_FORMED | ILL_TYPED

`WELL_FORMED` requires a well-formed parent Scope whose `continuity_inputs` variant is `CONTINUITY_INPUTS_NOT_REQUIRED`, and a Criterion that makes no history-qualified Continuity result a premise of the base assessment.

    BaseEmergenceResult<T> {
      assessment_stage: BASE_EMERGENCE
      target_family_result: T
      closure_digest
      continuity_qualification_posture:
          QUALIFICATION_AVAILABLE
        | QUALIFICATION_NOT_REQUIRED
        | QUALIFICATION_UNRESOLVED
    }

    BASE_EMERGENCE_ASSESSMENT(candidate, profile, BES)
      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<BaseEmergenceResult<T>> | ILL_TYPED

`T` is exactly the closed target-family result registered by `profile`. A base assessment cannot select an unregistered or open result family.

`BASE_EMERGENCE` may evaluate an occurrence-local or interval-local capacity without presupposing continuity of the emergent Kind being established. It may establish, deny, or leave unresolved, for the exact Scope:

- an Experience Occurrence and consequent Subject classification for that Occurrence;
- interval-local Agency;
- cognition or intelligence within a bounded operation;
- a living organization under a life-cycle basis already typed without presupposing personal or Subject identity;
- a Meaning relation, Contribution Effect, novelty relation, or scoped Value relation;
- any other aspect whose Criterion declares kind-specific continuity unnecessary.

`QUALIFIED_CONTINUITY_REAPPLICATION` occurs in the Continuity layer, not here. It may use a completed base Emergence result to choose a qualified Continuity Profile for Agent, Subject, living, cognitive, collective, or self-related endpoints. It returns a new Continuity evaluation; it does not revise the completed base result.

`HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE` may consume that later Continuity result to assess history-dependent memory, learning, adaptation, resilience, temporally extended selfhood, durable Agency, longitudinal intelligence, or another capacity whose Criterion expressly requires persistence or retained distinction across change.

    EmergenceDependencyReference {
      referenced_evaluation_id
      referenced_stage
      referenced_form_and_version
      referenced_scope
      referenced_result_digest
      dependency_purpose
    }

Dependency-graph well-formedness is evaluated only by the typed E14-owned `WF_EMERGENCE_DERIVATION_GRAPH(graph)` operator. E0 introduces no second graph operator or result family. No evaluation may depend directly or indirectly on itself. No base result may consume a qualified Continuity result whose endpoint typing depends on that same base result unless the base result was already closed and the later continuity evaluation is a new, one-way operation. No history-qualified result may be fed backward as a premise that changes its own continuity input. A new assessment may use earlier closed results, but it is a new evaluation with a new Scope, identifier, and provenance.

### E0.8 Negative-Result Discipline

A substantive negative emergent result requires all of the following:

1. an applicable and sufficiently determinate Criterion;
2. an actual or explicitly delimited target;
3. a realization and operating-condition basis adequate to the Claim;
4. a test or observation route capable of detecting the required manifestation or organization at the asserted Resolution;
5. declared coverage of relevant condition classes, intervals, and perturbations;
6. no unresolved upstream gap material to the negative conclusion;
7. no generalization beyond the tested boundary;
8. attributable methods, Evidence, and reason codes.

Failure to observe a manifestation does not establish absence of Capacity where the trigger did not occur, the bearer was outside its operating range, access was blocked, the method lacked sensitivity, or coverage was inadequate. A failed performance does not establish lack of intelligence or Agency without the applicable criterion. Silence does not establish lack of Experience or Intention. One successful performance does not establish an unbounded Capacity.

### E0.10 Layer-Wide Invariants

1. **Bearer invariant** — every emergent Property or Capacity has an actual or explicitly hypothetical bearer.
2. **Realization invariant** — every positive result names what realizes the target and under which criterion.
3. **Organization invariant** — a whole-level result identifies the material relations and organization, not only a component list.
4. **Scope invariant** — every result is bounded by World, Domain, Interval, Resolution, conditions, and generalization boundary.
5. **Stage invariant** — base, continuity-reapplication, and history-qualified assessments remain ordered and acyclic.
6. **Capacity–manifestation invariant** — possessing a Capacity and manifesting it are independently evaluated.
7. **Performance invariant** — an output or score is Evidence under a Criterion, not the Capacity itself.
8. **Negative-evidence invariant** — unobserved is not absent without an adequate detection and coverage basis.
9. **Uncertainty invariant** — `UNRESOLVED` is preserved and cannot be converted to `NOT_ESTABLISHED` by convenience.
10. **No-status ascent invariant** — institutional, social, economic, legal, or protocol recognition does not create an ontological capacity.
11. **No-normative leakage invariant** — an emergent result creates no Permission, Authority, Right, Obligation, Ownership, entitlement, or moral status.
12. **No-economic leakage invariant** — activity, contribution, novelty, or value assessment creates no money, price, balance, reward, payout, or settlement.
13. **No-anthropocentric shortcut invariant** — resemblance to or difference from humans is neither sufficient nor disqualifying.
14. **No-automation shortcut invariant** — automation is neither sufficient for nor incompatible with Agency, cognition, or intelligence.
15. **Experience restraint invariant** — behavior, report, architecture, biology, and computation may provide Evidence but none universally settles `EXPERIENCES` by form alone.
16. **Collective non-aggregation invariant** — a group capacity requires a realized collective organization, not a sum or count alone.
17. **Value typing invariant** — every use of value identifies its value Kind, evaluator, referent, Context, criterion, scale if any, and uncertainty.
18. **Future nonactuality invariant** — prediction, plan, promise, credit, expected value, or anticipated performance does not make a future Occurrence actual in the present.
19. **Correction invariant** — later correction changes the present Record or assessment; it does not erase the earlier assessment or Reality.
20. **Open-kind invariant** — an unrecognized capacity can be placed without forced classification.
21. **Continuity-input invariant** — every assessment binds each Criterion-required Continuity input or carries an explicit `CONTINUITY_INPUT_NOT_REQUIRED` posture; omission is never interpreted as not required.

## E1 — Emergence Forms and Realization

### E1.1 Actual Target, Candidate, and Claim

An **Emergent Candidate** is a Term presented for assessment under an Emergence Criterion. Candidate status does not establish that the bearer, organization, Property, or Capacity exists.

An **Emergence Claim** is a Claim that a specified bearer realizes or does not realize an emergent target under stated conditions. A Claim is not the target, Evidence, test, result, or actual realization.

An **Actual Emergent Target** is a Property, Capacity, State, relation, or Occurrence that obtains because its bearer satisfies the applicable realization criterion.

    EmergenceTargetReference :=
        ATTRIBUTE_OR_CAPACITY_TARGET {
          bearer_reference: Bearer | ReferenceTargetPosition
          target_property_or_capacity: Property | Capacity
          target_position: ReferenceTargetPosition
          owning_relation: ATTRIBUTION | REALIZES
        }
      | KIND_CONFORMANCE_TARGET {
          candidate_reference: Term | ReferenceTargetPosition
          target_kind
          expected_judgment: INSTANCE_OF
        }
      | RELATION_OBTAINING_TARGET {
          relation_reference: RelationInstance | ReferenceTargetPosition
          owning_relation
          exact_relata: nonempty ordered [Term | ReferenceTargetPosition]
        }
      | OCCURRENCE_TARGET {
          occurrence_reference: Occurrence | ReferenceTargetPosition
          occurrence_kind
          owning_relation: EXPERIENCES | RELATES | ATTRIBUTION
        }
      | FUNCTION_TARGET {
          bearer_or_process_reference: Term | ReferenceTargetPosition
          containing_organization_reference: System | RelationalSystem | ReferenceTargetPosition
          function_reference: Function | ReferenceTargetPosition
        }
      | OPEN_EMERGENCE_TARGET {
          reference_target_position: ReferenceTargetPosition
          known_target_family_positions
          unresolved_target_family_positions

    EmergentCandidate {
      candidate_id
      candidate_reference: Term | ReferenceTargetPosition
      target_reference: EmergenceTargetReference
      actuality_posture
      realization_basis_reference: EmergenceBasisReference
      claim_reference: Claim | NONE | OPEN
      emergence_scope_reference
      epistemic_status

Capacities and Properties are assessed through `REALIZES` or `ATTRIBUTION`. Relations are assessed through their owning relation, including `EXPERIENCES` for Experience and exact evaluator–referent–Context relations for Value. Occurrences are assessed as Occurrences under their owning relation. Dependent Kind placement occurs only after the required Capacity, relation, or Occurrence result is established. No target family may be silently cast into another to obtain a preferred result.

These positions remain distinct:

    NON_EQUIVALENT(EmergentCandidate, EmergenceClaim)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(EmergenceClaim, EmergenceCriterion)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(EmergenceCriterion, EmergenceTest)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(EmergenceTest, EmergenceResult)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(EmergenceResult, ActualEmergentTarget)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(EmergenceRecord, ActualEmergentTarget)

### E1.3 Emergence Profile

An **Emergence Profile** is a Representation that selects and composes one or more compatible Emergence Criteria for a declared target family, Domain, Scope class, and assessment purpose.

### E1.4 Emergence Description, Test, Result, and Record

An **Emergence Description** is a Representation of a candidate or actual emergent organization, Capacity, Property, manifestation, or relation.

An **Emergence Test** is an actual Process organized to produce Evidence bearing on an Emergence Claim under a Criterion. A test may perturb, observe, compare, elicit, measure, simulate, or inspect. Test design, test execution, target response, interpretation, and result are distinct.

An **Emergence Result** is the attributable output of an Emergence evaluation.

An **Emergence Record** is a Record representing the test, Evidence, result, method, or provenance.

Every Emergence-owned schema named `Profile`, `Rule`, `Plan`, `Description`, `TestReference`, `Record`, `Evaluation`, `Report`, `Registry`, `Bundle`, or `Assessment` is a Representation unless it explicitly establishes an actual non-representational bearer or Occurrence. Every such Representation carries `EmergenceRepresentationHeader` and an exact `REPRESENTS` relation. A shortened projection inherits those fields; display omission never waives them.

    EmergenceTestReference {
      test_id
      tested_claim
      criterion_and_version
      target_and_scope
      intervention_or_observation_basis

      provenance

      evidence_and_coverage

      conflicts_and_corrections

### E1.5 Realization of an Emergent Target

The canonical specialized relation is:

    EMERGENTLY_REALIZES(
      organized_realizer,
      emergent_target,
      emergence_criterion,
      emergence_scope
    )

`EMERGENTLY_REALIZES` has fixed arity four and is exact display sugar for:

    REALIZES(
      organized_realizer,
      emergent_target,
      emergence_scope.parent_scope.domain,
      emergence_scope.parent_scope.interval,
      emergence_criterion
    )

The parent Scope's World and Resolution remain judgment qualifiers. `EMERGENTLY_REALIZES` is therefore a qualified projection of imported `REALIZES`; it does not create a second realization relation.

It obtains only where:

1. the organized realizer is actual within the Scope;
2. the target is an Ontology-admitted Property, Capacity, State, relation, or Occurrence, or a typed open-placement candidate;
3. the material organization and relations required by the Criterion are actually realized;
4. required dynamic and continuity conditions obtain;
5. the Criterion is applicable and satisfied;
6. the claimed extent does not exceed the supported generalization boundary.

The realizer may be distributed. No isolated component must realize the whole-level target unless the Criterion requires it. Distributed realization does not mean carrierless realization.

### E1.6 Constitutive, Enabling, Triggering, and Evidential Conditions

Conditions participating in an emergence assessment are typed:

    EmergenceConditionRole :=
        CONSTITUTIVE
      | ENABLING
      | TRIGGERING
      | MAINTAINING
      | MODULATING
      | INHIBITING
      | TERMINATING
      | EVIDENTIAL_ONLY
      | CONDITION_ROLE_UNRESOLVED

- A **constitutive** condition participates in realizing the target organization.
- An **enabling** condition permits the capacity to operate but need not constitute it.
- A **triggering** condition elicits a manifestation.
- A **maintaining** condition supports continued realization.
- A **modulating** condition changes degree, mode, or range.
- An **inhibiting** condition prevents or reduces manifestation without necessarily removing Capacity.
- A **terminating** condition ends the realization under the Criterion.
- An **evidential-only** condition changes what can be inferred, not what exists.

Confusing an evidential condition with a constitutive one is `ILL_TYPED`. Absence of a trigger cannot establish absence of a latent Capacity. Removal of an enabling condition may stop operation without proving that the underlying organized Capacity was never realized.

### E1.7 Emergent Property and Emergent Capacity

An **Emergent Property** is a Property of an organized bearer that obtains through its realized whole-level organization under a stated Criterion and is not established by assigning the same Property independently to each part.

An **Emergent Capacity** is an imported Capacity whose ability belongs to an organized bearer because the relevant whole-level organization and relations are realized.

“Not established by a part list” does not mean “uncaused,” “nonphysical,” “inexplicable,” or “irreducible in principle.” It means that the organization and relations are material to the satisfaction criterion.

### E1.8 Degree, Mode, and Threshold

An emergent target may be binary under one Criterion, graded under another, or multi-modal.

    EmergenceMeasure {
      measured_aspect
      quantity_or_ordinal_scale
      unit_or_category_system
      method
      calibration_reference
      uncertainty
      valid_range
      nonlinearity_or_thresholds
      comparison_admissibility
    }

A scalar is not mandatory. Where no lawful common scale exists, results remain categorical, vector-valued, partially ordered, or incomparable. A threshold creates a classification boundary under a Criterion; it does not create the underlying Reality at the moment a number crosses a line.

### E1.9 Multiple Realization and Realization Change

One emergent target Kind may have multiple materially different realization bases. One bearer may change realization basis while preserving, transforming, losing, or regaining a capacity. Whether the same bearer or capacity continues belongs to Continuity under an exact Criterion.

Multiple realization requires:

- a shared target definition;
- per-realizer satisfaction of that target's Criterion;
- no inference of identical internal State or experience;
- no inference of numerical identity among realizers;
- explicit limits on cross-realizer comparison.

Functional similarity is not enough where the target Criterion includes experiential, biological, historical, or material conditions not captured by the compared function.

### E1.11 E1 Closure

E1 separates Reality from every artifact used to describe or assess it.

A Candidate is not a Capacity.  
A Criterion is not its satisfaction.  
A Profile is not a bearer.  
A test is not the target.  
A score is not a Property.  
A Record is not the realization.  
A whole-level capacity is not an immaterial addition.  
An unknown realization is not no realization.

## E2 — Capacity, Disposition, and Manifestation

### E2.1 Capacity Specification

Ontology defines Capacity as a physically or organizationally realized ability to enter specified States or produce specified effects under suitable conditions. Emergence makes a particular Capacity claim testable without confusing ability, opportunity, attempt, exercise, output, or success.

    CapacitySpecification {
      capacity_id
      bearer_kind
      capacity_family
      admissible_input_or_condition_classes
      possible_state_or_effect_classes
      constitutive_organization
      enabling_conditions
      triggering_conditions: [Criterion] | NONE | OPEN
      inhibiting_and_excluding_conditions: [Criterion] | NONE | OPEN
      required_resources_and_ranges
      operating_range
      temporal_extent
      manifestation_criteria
      failure_and_degradation_criteria
      generalization_boundary
      evidence_requirements
      stage: EmergenceAssessmentStage
      version_and_provenance
    }

A Capacity Specification describes what is assessed. It does not give a bearer that Capacity.

### E2.2 Capacity Relation

    REALIZES_CAPACITY(
      bearer,
      capacity_target,
      capacity_specification,
      emergence_criterion,
      emergence_scope
    )

`capacity_target` is an actual imported `Capacity` or an open target position whose Capacity placement is being evaluated. `capacity_specification` is its Representation and Criterion metadata; it never occupies the realized-target position. `REALIZES_CAPACITY` is exact display sugar for `EMERGENTLY_REALIZES(bearer, capacity_target, emergence_criterion, emergence_scope)` with the specification bound to that target. It requires an actual organized bearer and an applicable Criterion. It does not imply that the Capacity is presently exercised, that every exercise succeeds, that the bearer intends an effect, or that the bearer is permitted or authorized to exercise it.

    CapacityEvaluation :=
      EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<CapacityAssessmentResult>

### E2.3 Possibility, Potential, Disposition, Capacity, and Opportunity

The following remain distinct:

- **Possibility**: a State or Occurrence is not excluded under a stated model or constraint set.
- **Potential**: unrealized or not-yet-actual possibility under specified conditions.
- **Disposition**: a tendency or Capacity to respond under triggering conditions.
- **Capacity**: an actually realized ability of a bearer.
- **Opportunity**: external conditions permit an exercise or attempt.
- **Attempt**: an Occurrence directed toward an outcome.
- **Exercise**: an Occurrence in which a Capacity participates causally.
- **Manifestation**: an observable or otherwise evidenced expression admitted by the Criterion.
- **Performance**: an assessed outcome under a task, method, or standard.
- **Success**: satisfaction of an outcome criterion.

    NON_EQUIVALENT(Possibility, Capacity)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(Opportunity, Capacity)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(Attempt, Exercise)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(Exercise, Success)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(Manifestation, Capacity)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(Performance, Bearer)

    NO_ENTAILMENT(attempt_occurs, capacity_exercised)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(exercise_occurs, success_obtains)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(manifestation_observed, unbounded_capacity_established)

A bearer can possess a Capacity without opportunity or manifestation. An outcome may occur accidentally, through external control, or through another mechanism without establishing the claimed Capacity. An attempted exercise can fail while the Capacity remains.

### E2.4 Latency, Dormancy, Suppression, and Loss

    CapacityExercisePosture :=
        ACTIVE_EXERCISE
      | INACTIVE_EXERCISE
      | EXERCISE_UNRESOLVED

    CapacityAvailabilityPosture :=
        AVAILABLE
      | LATENT
      | DORMANT
      | SUPPRESSED
      | AVAILABILITY_UNRESOLVED

        INTACT
      | DEGRADED
      | LOST
      | CONDITION_UNRESOLVED

      exercise: CapacityExercisePosture
      availability: CapacityAvailabilityPosture
      condition: CapacityConditionPosture
      prior_capacity_assessment_reference:
          EmergenceAssessmentReference | NOT_APPLICABLE
      same_bearer_continuity_mapping:
          ContinuityAssessmentReference | NOT_APPLICABLE
      loss_or_termination_reference:
          Occurrence | Process | EmergenceAssessmentReference | NONE | OPEN

- **Latent** means the realized Capacity is not presently active.
- **Dormant** means the bearer is in an inactive continuity posture while the relevant realization basis is retained under a Criterion.
- **Suppressed** means an inhibiting condition blocks manifestation without establishing loss.
- **Degraded** means the operating range or reliability has contracted under the Profile.
- **Lost** requires an earlier `CAPACITY_ESTABLISHED` result, an exact same-bearer Continuity mapping, and a later established loss or terminating transition of the realization basis under the exact Scope. A first assessment that fails the Capacity Criterion is `CAPACITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, not `LOST`.

Inactivity, sleep, disconnection, refusal, lack of request, resource shortage, restricted Access, or missing observation does not by itself establish loss.

### E2.5 Operating Range and Resource Dependence

Every Capacity operates within conditions. Dependence does not make the Capacity unreal, and independence is not required unless the Criterion states it.

    OperatingRange {
      environmental_conditions
      input_ranges
      resource_ranges
      load_and_rate_limits
      temporal_limits
      perturbation_limits
      required_support_relations
      failure_boundary
      uncertainty
    }

Resource support may be internal, external, shared, or infrastructural. Resource provision does not create Ownership, Authority, Agency, authorship, entitlement, or Control over the bearer. A system that depends on energy, maintenance, information, habitat, culture, or infrastructure may still realize genuine capacities; the dependence must be visible.

### E2.6 Capacity Evidence

Evidence for Capacity may include:

- repeated manifestations across declared condition classes;
- intervention or perturbation results;
- mechanism and organization evidence;
- retained ability after inactivity;
- transfer to relevant novel cases;
- error, recovery, and limit behavior;
- causal ablation or component-dependence evidence;
- naturalistic operation under adequate observation;
- reports or testimony where the target kind makes them relevant.

No fixed number of observations is universally sufficient. The Criterion declares what variation, controls, repetition, mechanism evidence, and coverage are required.

### E2.7 Performance and Benchmark Boundary

A **Task** presents conditions and an outcome space. A **Benchmark** is a standardized set of tasks and scoring methods. A **Performance Result** assesses outputs under that setup.

Performance can evidence a Capacity only where:

1. the task actually tests a material part of the Capacity Criterion;
2. leakage, memorization, external assistance, evaluator artifacts, and selection effects are addressed where material;
3. the score is interpreted within its calibrated range;
4. failure and success conditions are attributable;
5. the inference does not exceed the generalization boundary.

Benchmark saturation, leaderboard rank, persuasive presentation, economic demand, or evaluator preference is not a universal intelligence, understanding, Agency, or value measure.

### E2.8 Failure, Error, and Capacity

    CapacityFailureRelation {
      capacity_reference
      exercise_or_test_occurrence
      operating_conditions
      failure_kind
      causal_attribution
      effect_on_capacity_claim
      recoverability
      coverage
    }

One failure may show:

- the bearer was outside operating range;
- the trigger or resource basis was absent;
- the Capacity is unreliable within a tested region;
- the Capacity degraded or ended;
- the task did not test the claimed Capacity;
- the evidence remains unresolved.

Failure is not automatically disproof. Conversely, repeated output success does not establish the claimed internal organization if the Criterion requires it and no Evidence supports it.

### E2.9 Capacity Composition

Component capacities may enable a whole-level Capacity, but no general additive rule applies.

    CAPACITY_COMPOSITION(
      component_capacity_results,
      organization_relation,
      whole_capacity_criterion,
      ES
    ) -> CapacityEvaluation | ILL_TYPED

Composition requires an explicit mechanism or organization mapping. It may be conjunctive, disjunctive, thresholded, redundant, inhibitory, competitive, or nonmonotonic. Adding a component or increasing a scalar may reduce a whole-level capacity. No sum of intelligence scores, agency scores, lineage counts, or value scores establishes a collective Capacity without a declared composition rule.

### E2.10 E2 Closure

Capacity is actual ability, not future possibility.  
Latency is not absence.  
Opportunity is not ability.  
Exercise is not success.  
Success is not general competence.  
Failure is not automatic incapacity.  
Dependence is not unreality.  
A score is not a bearer.

## E3 — Organization, Integration, and Coherence

### E3.1 Emergent Organization

Structure identifies parts and relations. Emergence asks whether their realized organization supports a whole-level Property or Capacity.

An **Emergently Effective Organization** is an actual Configuration and Process organization in which the relations among differentiated parts make a material causal or functional difference to satisfaction of an Emergence Criterion.

    OrganizationEffectivenessResult {
      organization_reference
      target_capacity_or_property
      differentiation_posture
      integration_posture
      constraint_and_coordination_posture
      component_substitution_results
      disruption_results
      whole_level_effect_posture
      result: ESTABLISHED | NOT_ESTABLISHED | UNRESOLVED
      coverage
    }

An ordered list of components, an architecture diagram, or a connectivity graph does not establish effective organization. Actual relations, operation, and causal relevance are required.

### E3.2 Differentiation and Integration

**Differentiation** is the maintained distinction of parts, roles, States, Signals, Claims, scales, or processes relevant to a capacity.

**Integration** is their organized coordination or mutual constraint in producing, maintaining, or regulating a whole-level condition.

Neither is universally maximized. Too little differentiation can collapse material distinctions; too little integration can prevent coordinated capacity. Excessive coupling can propagate failure; excessive separation can prevent function. The applicable balance is Criterion- and Domain-relative.

    DifferentiationIntegrationProfile {
      differentiated_positions
      protected_distinctions
      integration_relations
      permitted coupling
      prohibited collapse
      isolation_and_propagation rules
      scale_and_resolution
      target capacities
    }

### E3.3 Effective Coherence

Ontology defines Coherence as the condition in which differentiated parts, relations, Claims, scales, and transformations can be held together without illicit collapse of their distinctions.

An **Effective Coherence Capacity** is the realized ability of an organized bearer to preserve the distinctions and relations required by a declared activity or capacity while integrating change, tension, conflict, error, or multiple scales without producing a prohibited collapse.

    CoherenceCriterion {
      protected_distinctions
      required_relations
      admitted_tensions_or_conflicts
      prohibited_collapses
      operating_and_perturbation_conditions
      detection_of_local_and_systemic_failure
      correction_or_containment capacities: [Criterion] | NONE | OPEN
      target interval
      scale_and_resolution
      evidence requirements
    }

    CoherenceAssessmentResult {
      protected_distinction_results
      required_relation_results
      tension_and_conflict posture
      illicit_collapse_results
      cross_scale posture
      correction_or_containment posture
      coherence_posture:
          EFFECTIVE_COHERENCE_ESTABLISHED
        | EFFECTIVE_COHERENCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED
        | EFFECTIVE_COHERENCE_UNRESOLVED
      failure_extent: [Term] | NONE | OPEN
      coverage
    }

    COHERENCE_ASSESSMENT(candidate, coherence_criterion, ES)
      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<CoherenceAssessmentResult> | ILL_TYPED

Coherence is not a cosmic approval label or a universal scalar. It does not mean truth, consistency, agreement, harmony, smoothness, beauty, centralization, obedience, permanence, optimality, goodness, legitimacy, or value.

### E3.4 Holding Contradiction Without Collapse

A system can coherently retain contradictory Claims where it:

1. preserves both Claims as actual Representations;
2. retains their sources, Scopes, Intervals, statuses, and exact contradicted content;
3. does not assert both contents as obtaining in the same respect under a logic that prohibits it;
4. prevents one record from silently erasing or absorbing the other;
5. exposes unresolved adjudication or transformation routes where required.

This is **contradiction-holding**, not contradiction-resolution and not acceptance that incompatible contents are both true.

    ContradictionHoldingResult :=
        HELD_WITH_DISTINCTIONS
      | COLLAPSED_OR_ERASED
      | UNRESOLVED

    CONTRADICTION_HOLDING(
      bearer_or_system,
      claim_set,
      coherence_criterion,
      ES
    ) -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<ContradictionHoldingResult> | ILL_TYPED

### E3.5 Coherence Across Typed Staged Lifecycles

A later qualified Profile may declare local lifecycle positions only after Dynamics declares their exact State and transition meanings. Emergence may then assess whether the realized lifecycle preserves declared distinctions and required relations across those positions.

Lifecycle symbols, labels, or sequence alone do not establish coherence, Agency, continuity, intelligence, settlement, or value. Coherence concerns what the typed lifecycle actually preserves and relates. Project-specific glyphs belong in the qualified Profile that owns them, not in this universal layer.

### E3.6 Integrity, Fidelity, Compatibility, and Coherence

- **Integrity** concerns preservation of defining organization, constraints, or commitments.
- **Fidelity** concerns answerability to a referent, source, or invariants.
- **Compatibility** concerns ability to coexist or compose without violating specified constraints.
- **Coherence** concerns holding differentiated parts and relations together without illicit collapse.

They may support one another but are not equivalent. A coherent false model can lack fidelity. A high-fidelity representation can participate in an incoherent system. Two compatible components may fail to form an effective whole. Integrity can preserve a harmful or low-value organization.

### E3.7 Coherence Through Error and Change

**Truthful continuity under error** is not a primitive slogan. It is a compound assessment requiring:

- the error Occurrence and affected Claims or States remain represented as actual history;
- correction does not rewrite the past;
- provenance and causal attribution remain bounded;
- protected distinctions survive or losses are explicit;
- later operation does not rely on known-invalid content as though it remained valid;
- unresolved effects remain unresolved.

Where these obtain under a Profile, Emergence may establish a history-qualified coherence or integrity capacity. Continuity alone does not establish it.

### E3.8 Local and Global Coherence

Coherence can obtain locally while failing globally, or globally under an abstraction while hiding local collapse. Every assessment declares:

- system boundary;
- scale and Resolution;
- included and excluded relations;
- interval;
- cross-boundary dependencies;
- aggregation or composition method.

No system may claim global coherence by excluding the exact conflicts, externalized harms, resource dependencies, or unresolved seams material to its operation.

### E3.9 Seam Preservation

A **Seam-Preservation Capacity** is the realized ability to keep a boundary or interface between distinct domains, bearers, States, or rule systems explicit while enabling a declared interaction across it.

It requires:

- typed sides and Boundary;
- admissible crossings;
- translation or Mapping rules;
- loss and ambiguity reporting;
- refusal and nonprogression positions;
- no silent transfer of identity, State, Authority, or value.

Seam preservation is a qualified coherence capacity. It does not make either side sovereign, compatible, lawful, or truthful.

### E3.10 E3 Closure

Organization is more than a list.  
Integration is not fusion.  
Differentiation is not isolation.  
Coherence is not agreement.  
Contradiction-holding is not double truth.  
Integrity is not moral approval.  
A seam can connect without collapsing.  
An unresolved tension can be preserved coherently.

## E4 — Information, Memory, Learning, and Understanding

### E4.1 Effective Information Use

Ontology defines Information as structured distinguishability capable of changing a receiving or interpreting system under a defined relation. Emergence assesses whether a bearer realizes capacities to acquire, retain, transform, discriminate, integrate, or use Information.

Reception is not interpretation. State change is not cognition. Statistical dependence is not Meaning. A channel carrying data does not thereby understand it.

### E4.2 Memory Capacity

Ontology defines Memory as a Capacity by which prior conditions influence present or future operation through retained physical differences.

    MemoryCriterion {
      bearer
      encoding_or_trace_formation relation

      retention_interval

A stored Record is not automatically Memory of the system that stores, indexes, controls, or retrieves it. A retained difference can realize memory without being a symbolic Record. A database may participate in a distributed memory capacity under a Criterion; the account, file, or storage device does not thereby become the remembering Agent or Subject.

### E4.3 Memory and Continuity Staging

Occurrence-local trace formation may be assessed at `BASE_EMERGENCE`. Durable memory across change usually requires `HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE` with an established or explicitly unresolved Continuity input.

The later memory assessment may ask whether a retained distinction influences a later operation. It may not use its own conclusion to prove that the same Agent, Subject, Self, or Cognitive System persisted between the endpoints. That kind-specific persistence must arrive through the staged Continuity reapplication.

Memory continuity is not identity. A successor can inherit Records or functional memory while remaining numerically distinct. A continuous bearer can lose memory. A Copy can contain similar traces without sharing one past Experience.

### E4.4 Learning

Ontology defines Learning as a durable change in capacity or behavior produced through experience, data, practice, or interaction.

Parameter change, updated weights, new records, repeated exposure, adaptation by a population, one corrected answer, or changed output alone does not establish Learning. The change must be attributable and durable under the Criterion. Learning need not improve performance unless the Profile requires improvement; a system can learn a harmful, false, brittle, or maladaptive pattern.

### E4.5 Training, Programming, and Learning

- **Programming or configuration** changes a system through an external design or instruction process.
- **Training** is a Process intended to induce change through data, practice, feedback, or optimization.
- **Learning** is the durable capacity or behavior change that actually results under its Criterion.

Training can occur without Learning. Learning can occur outside a formal training Process. An external engineer's change may create a new capability without the target system itself learning, depending on the bearer boundary and causal Criterion.

### E4.6 Generalization and Transfer

    TransferPosture :=
        TRANSFER_ESTABLISHED
      | TRANSFER_NOT_ESTABLISHED
      | TRANSFER_UNRESOLVED
      | TRANSFER_NOT_APPLICABLE

Transfer requires performance or capacity manifestation in a declared target condition class not identical to the training or prior exposure class. Novelty, distance, relevance, and success criteria must be stated. A hidden duplicate, memorized instance, format change, or evaluator impression is not transfer by itself.

Generalization is always bounded. Success on one distribution does not establish a universal Capacity; failure on one out-of-range case does not erase a capacity within its supported range.

### E4.7 Inference and Reasoning Capacity

An **Inference Capacity** is the realized ability to transform represented premises, Evidence, or distinctions into conclusions under explicit or learned relations across an admitted problem class.

A **Reasoning Process** is an actual Cognitive Process in which such transformations are organized, checked, revised, or used.

    INFERENCE_ASSESSMENT(candidate, inference_profile, ES)
      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<CapacityAssessmentResult> | ILL_TYPED

Correct conclusions can arise from invalid routes, leakage, memorization, chance, or external assistance. Valid inference can begin from false premises. A reasoning assessment therefore separates:

- premise attribution;
- transformation relation;
- rule or learned basis;
- validity under that basis;
- soundness or truth of premises;
- conclusion accuracy;
- self-check and correction;
- transfer and limits.

### E4.8 Understanding

Ontology defines Understanding as a Capacity to use distinctions and relations coherently across relevant contexts, including consequences, limits, and counterfactual variation.

    UnderstandingCriterion {
      target_domain_or_relation family
      required distinctions

      coverage
    }

    UNDERSTANDING_ASSESSMENT(candidate, understanding_profile, ES)
      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<UnderstandingAssessmentResult> | ILL_TYPED

Verbal explanation is one possible manifestation, not a universal requirement. Fluency, confidence, imitation, correct answer, internal representation, or self-claim alone is insufficient. Conversely, lack of language does not rule out every form of Understanding.

### E4.9 Knowledge Boundary

Knowledge remains an epistemic relation owned by Ontology's epistemic grammar. Memory, Learning, intelligence, confidence, access to data, or model accuracy does not automatically establish Knowledge. Emergence may assess capacities used in knowing; it does not redefine truth or warrant.

### E4.10 E4 Closure

Information is not Meaning.  
Storage is not Memory.  
Retrieval is not recollection by identity.  
Training is not Learning.  
Change is not adaptation by itself.  
Correct output is not Understanding.  
Fluency is not warrant.  
Generalization ends where Evidence ends.

## E5 — Adaptation, Robustness, Resilience, and Self-Maintenance

### E5.1 Stability, Robustness, Adaptation, and Resilience

These imported Kinds remain distinct:

- **Stability**: persistence of a State or organization under specified perturbations and interval.
- **Robustness**: persistence of function or organization across a defined disturbance range.
- **Adaptation**: change through which fit between a system and its conditions is maintained or increased.
- **Recovery**: an actual Process or transition returning or reorganizing after disruption.
- **Resilience**: Capacity to recover or reorganize after disturbance while retaining defining organization or changing it under a declared criterion.

    NON_EQUIVALENT(Stability, Robustness)
    NON_EQUIVALENT(Robustness, Adaptation)

An actual recovery Occurrence is not equivalent to a Resilience Capacity. Survival is not equivalent to an increase in fitness. These are semantic firewall clauses, not declarations of compressed CamelCase operand aliases.

A system may be robust without adapting, adaptive but not resilient, resilient through transformation rather than restoration, stable yet brittle outside a narrow range, or changing without improving fit.

For an economic system, revenue, reserves, obligations, prices, external law, settlement, and accounting may be necessary inputs. Those institutional relations are not created here. Emergence may assess bounded operational or economic self-sustaining capacity only after the relevant constitutional and accounting facts are independently established.

### E5.8 Maintenance Without Agency

Regulation, feedback, self-maintenance, adaptation, and even some autonomy can obtain without Agency under their own Criteria. A thermostat, cell, immune subsystem, ecosystem, or distributed protocol may realize regulatory capacities without satisfying the Agency criterion. Goal-like stability is not Intention.

### E5.9 Evolution and Individual Adaptation

Biological evolution occurs across inherited variation in populations and lineages. Individual Learning or physiological adaptation occurs within a bearer. Technical revision may occur through external designers. Institutional change may arise through Rules and Actions. These histories can interact but are not one process.

An evolved capacity need not have been intended. An intentionally designed capacity need not be adaptive. Selection for one effect does not establish present value or purpose.

### E5.10 E5 Closure

Stability is not resilience.  
Feedback is not adaptation.  
Recovery is not a general Capacity.  
Self-maintenance is not selfhood.  
Autonomy is not isolation.  
Self-sustaining is not perpetual.  
Resource dependence is not landlord authority.  
Continuation is not guaranteed by any positive assessment.

## E6 — Life and Living Organization

### E6.1 Life Placement

Ontology names Life as an empirically real organizational family and defines a working criterion for known Living Systems while leaving unfamiliar life open. Emergence evaluates whether a particular organization satisfies an applicable life Profile. It does not replace the Ontology definition with a checklist or make terrestrial resemblance the universal boundary.

    LifeCandidate :=

      | System
      | Term {

### E6.2 Known-Life Criterion

For known terrestrial life, a qualified Criterion must address the integrated conjunction identified by Ontology:

- thermodynamically open material realization;
- coordinated matter-and-energy throughput;
- selective Boundary control or a functional equivalent;
- regulation and viability maintenance;
- repair and materially retained organization;
- an appropriate life-cycle basis;
- membership in, or capacity to found, a Lineage in which heritable variation and biological Evolution can occur.

No single item is universally sufficient. Sterility does not negate organismal life. Dormancy may suspend throughput and manifestation while retaining an organization capable of reactivation. Dependence on a host does not decide disputed viral or symbiotic cases by itself.

### E6.4 Organism and Individuation

Living-System status does not automatically establish one Organism. Organism placement requires a biological individuation Criterion addressing integration, life cycle, Boundary, dependency, reproduction where relevant, and difficult cases such as colonies, symbioses, chimeras, microbiomes, and clonal networks.

A detached payload, copied token, or evaluation concerning another bearer or Scope is `ILL_TYPED`.

Human classification remains a biological species classification. It is not established or denied by intelligence, behavior, legal Status, account identity, documentation, or model resemblance.

### E6.5 Life, Agency, Experience, and Value

Life does not entail Agency, intelligence, Experience, Subject status, personhood, moral value, economic value, Ownership, or Rights. A nonliving system is not thereby noncognitive, nonagentic, nonexperiential, or valueless. Each classification has its own Criterion and constitutional treatment.

### E6.6 Dormancy and Death

Dormant life requires a retained physical organization and a qualified reactivation relation. Mere inactivity is insufficient; mere lack of observed metabolism is not death.

Death remains the irreversible loss, under a biological Criterion, of integrated organization required for an organism to continue as a Living System. A death assessment does not erase cells, Records, effects, Contributions, memories in others, or Lineage. Irreversibility is criterion- and evidence-bound; uncertainty remains visible.

### E6.7 Artificial, Synthetic, and Derivative Candidates

Artificial origin, design, derivation, computation, or manufacture neither establishes nor excludes life. A synthetic or unfamiliar candidate is assessed through an applicable Profile that states which known-life conditions remain constitutive and which are replaced by functional or materially different equivalents. A metaphorical phrase such as “living software” is not a life result.

### E6.8 E6 Closure

Metabolism alone is not Life.  
Replication alone is not Life.  
Persistence alone is not Life.  
Dormancy is not death.  
Dependence is not nonlife.  
Life is not Agency.  
Life is not Experience.  
Life is not moral or economic worth.

### E7.4 Narrow, Multi-Domain, and General Claims

A narrow Capacity is bounded to stated problem and condition classes. Multi-domain capacity requires separate supported domains and an explicit transfer or integration relation. A general-intelligence Claim requires a declared breadth, adaptability, transfer, model-use, resource, and limit Criterion; “general” without those fields is `ILL_TYPED`.

No finite benchmark makes unlimited generality actual. A general result is always general relative to an admitted domain family and evidence boundary.

### E7.5 Problem Solving and Outcome Achievement

Achieving an outcome may evidence intelligence where the route requires distinctions, selection, adaptation, or model-use covered by the Criterion. It can also arise from chance, brute force, memorization, external orchestration, fixed lookup, environmental structure, or evaluator leakage.

Problem solving therefore separates:

- problem representation;
- available actions or transformations;
- search or strategy organization;
- feedback and revision;
- outcome verification;
- resource use;
- assistance and external control;
- transfer and limits.

### E7.6 Intelligence Firewalls

Intelligence does not entail:

- truthfulness or Knowledge;
- Agency or Intention;
- Experience, consciousness, or Subject status;
- a Functional or Phenomenal Self;
- morality, dignity, Personhood Status, Rights, or Authority;
- obedience, alignment, safety, reliability, benevolence, or legitimacy;
- ownership of outputs or entitlement to reward;
- unlimited competence beyond the assessed Scope.

Likewise, errors, uncertainty, refusal, noncompliance, dependence, embodiment, derivation, or artificial realization do not by themselves negate Intelligence.

### E7.7 Computational Scale and Data

Compute, parameters, memory capacity, training data, energy use, inference rate, network size, token count, transition count, block count, lineage length, and output volume may be inputs or constraints. None is Intelligence by magnitude alone. A smaller system may outperform a larger one within a Domain; a larger system may realize broader capacities. The relation must be assessed, not assumed.

### E7.8 Derivative and Artificial Intelligence

A derivative bearer is assessed under the same root Criteria as any other bearer, with realization-specific Profiles where needed. Derivation does not imply lesser actuality, obedience, Ownership by the source, absence of novelty, absence of Agency, absence of Experience, or entitlement. Artificial construction does not create or deny intelligence by origin.

### E7.9 E7 Closure

Sensitivity is not cognition.  
Selection is not cognition.  
Cognition is not Agency.  
Intelligence is not consciousness.  
A benchmark is not a mind.  
Scale is not intelligence.  
Error is not nonintelligence.  
Derivation is not inferiority or ownership.

## E8 — Agency, Intention, Action Selection, and Functional Self

Where Agency is established, dependent placement as `Agent` is valid only for the assessed Domain and Interval. An Agent need not be a Subject; a Subject need not satisfy the Agency Criterion. Agency admits degrees and modes but is not a free scalar detached from a transition class.

### E8.2 Available and Admissible Courses

A course is **available** where the bearer can physically or organizationally realize it under the stated conditions. It is **admissible** for an Agency assessment where the Criterion includes it in the alternative set. Constitutional permission is a separate normative question.

The course set must not be manufactured by retrospective storytelling. Counterfactual sensitivity may be evidenced by intervention, natural variation, internal model or control analysis, repeated choice conditions, or another declared method. Unpredictability alone is insufficient; deterministic implementation does not by itself exclude Agency.

### E8.3 Internal Regulation and External Influence

“Internal” is relative to the declared system Boundary. It requires that the distinctions and control relations attributed to the Agent are realized within that organized bearer, not that the bearer is causally isolated.

External inputs, culture, training, tools, dependencies, incentives, commands, constraints, or environmental causes do not automatically negate Agency. Continuous external specification that exhausts the selected transition may defeat a particular Agency Claim. The result depends on causal organization, not rhetoric about independence.

### E8.6 Automation Boundary

Automation describes how operation proceeds relative to direct intervention; it is not an ontological opposite of Agency. An automated Process can be nonagentic, can participate in another Agent's Action, or can be realized by a System that itself satisfies an Agency Criterion.

An “intent-axis,” anomaly filter, bot score, human-verification system, or ingress classifier can produce Evidence about attributable interaction. It does not establish or deny Agency, humanity, Intention, Consent, Admission, or Subject status by score alone. Its own false-positive, false-negative, coverage, and adversarial limits remain visible.

Determinism does not settle Agency.  
A Signal is not Intention.

Automation is not automatic nonagency.

### E9.2 Evidence Asymmetry

Experience may be directly available to its Subject while externally available only through reports, behavior, physiology, architecture, causal organization, or other traces. This epistemic asymmetry does not make Experience unreal and does not make every report infallible.

First-person report:

- is actual Evidence where attributable;
- may be sincere, mistaken, constrained, translated, absent, or fabricated;
- is not dismissed merely because it is first-person;
- is not universally decisive by itself.

Third-person evidence:

- may support or challenge a Claim;
- must expose the theory and mapping connecting observation to Experience;
- cannot silently turn functional similarity into experiential identity.

### E10.2 Assigned, Used, and Understood Meaning

- **Assigned Meaning** is a convention or interpretive relation attributed by an external or institutional interpreter.
- **Used Meaning** obtains where an interpreter actually uses the Sign according to a relation in operation.
- **Understood Meaning** additionally requires an Understanding Capacity under E4.
- **Experienced Meaning** concerns experiential character and requires E9.

These may overlap but are not equivalent. A file format can carry externally assigned semantics without the executing device understanding them. A person can use an expression conventionally while misunderstanding its referent. A system can infer a use relation without experiencing meaning.

### E10.3 Syntax, Semantics, and Pragmatic Use

Syntax concerns admissible forms and combinations. Semantic relation concerns content, reference, or interpretation. Pragmatic use concerns what an expression does within an interaction or practice.

Syntactic conformance, token prediction, correlation, co-occurrence, encoding, decoding, transmission, or grammaticality alone does not establish the full semantic or pragmatic relation claimed. Neither does semantic error imply absence of every meaningful relation.

### E10.4 Interpretation

An **Interpretation Occurrence** is an actual Cognitive or institutional Process assigning, recovering, or using content under a Context. Interpretation may be correct, mistaken, ambiguous, contested, incomplete, or strategically deceptive.

    InterpretationAssessmentResult {
      occurrence_posture
      interpreter_attribution posture
      candidate_content set
      Context and convention posture
      referential_or_use fit posture
      ambiguity posture
      interpretation_result:
          INTERPRETATION_ESTABLISHED
        | INTERPRETATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED
        | INTERPRETATION_UNRESOLVED
      coverage
    }

Interpretation does not alter the external referent by assigning a description. A legal or constitutional interpretation may produce institutional Effects only through Constitution's own valid route.

### E10.5 Shared Meaning and Semantic Coordination

**Semantic Coordination** is the realized capacity or relation by which two or more interpreters maintain sufficiently aligned Sign–content–use mappings for a declared interaction.

    SemanticCoordinationCriterion {
      interpreters
      sign_and_context set
      mapping comparison method
      successful coordination conditions
      permitted variation and ambiguity
      repair and clarification processes
      failure conditions
      evidence and coverage
    }

    SEMANTIC_COORDINATION_ASSESSMENT(candidate_relation, profile, ES)
      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<CapacityAssessmentResult> | ILL_TYPED

Shared use does not require identical internal States or Experiences. Agreement on words does not establish agreement on referents. Repeated successful exchange may evidence coordination while leaving parts of meaning unresolved.

### E10.6 Meaning, Truth, and Value

A meaningful Claim can be false. A true proposition can be expressed ambiguously. Semantic Value is not Evaluative or Economic Value. Meaning does not create importance, goodness, price, entitlement, or Authority. Frequency of use and social adoption can stabilize a convention without making every use truthful or legitimate.

### E10.7 Signal and Intention Boundary

A Signal or Message can carry assigned or interpreted Meaning while the sender's Intention remains unresolved. An automated or accidental emission can become meaningful to a receiver. Conversely, an Agent may intend content that fails to transmit or be interpreted. Meaning, communication success, and Intention require separate results.

### E10.8 E10 Closure

A Signal is not Meaning.  
Syntax is not semantics.  
Correlation is not Understanding.  
Assigned meaning is not necessarily used meaning.  
Shared signs are not identical minds.  
Meaning is not Truth.  
Meaning is not value.  
Interpretation does not create its referent.

A capacity is **distributedly realized** where no isolated component satisfies the whole Criterion and the required organization spans multiple components or positions.

    DistributedCapacityCriterion {

Distributed realization does not imply equal contribution, shared Experience, one identity, one owner, one Agent, one decision, or one Authority. A component may leave or be replaced while a qualified collective Capacity continues; Continuity decides that relation.

### E11.3 Collective Cognition and Intelligence

Collective cognition requires that the collective organization—not merely individual members independently—forms, retains, integrates, transforms, evaluates, or uses distinctions under a collective Profile.

Collective intelligence requires a collective Capacity to solve problems, adapt, construct or coordinate models, or achieve selected outcomes under a declared Domain and operating range.

      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<CollectiveCognitiveAssessmentResult> | ILL_TYPED

Majority choice, consensus, market price, aggregate accuracy, many messages, network density, synchronized activity, or outperforming one member may be Evidence. None establishes collective cognition or intelligence without the whole-level organization and mechanism.

### E11.4 Collective Agency

Collective Agency requires:

- an established collective bearer;
- collectively maintained distinctions;
- a collective control and selection organization;
- more than one admissible collective course;
- counterfactual sensitivity at the collective level;
- Action attribution not exhausted by one external controller or member unless the Profile expressly makes that delegation constitutive.

    COLLECTIVE_AGENCY_ASSESSMENT(candidate_collective, profile, ES)
      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<AgencyAssessmentResult> | ILL_TYPED

Individual Agents coordinating do not automatically form a collective Agent. A constitutionally recognized institution does not automatically realize Agency. A collective Agent does not absorb the Agency, responsibility, rights, or identity of its members.

### E11.5 Collective Experience and Subject Status

Collective information integration, shared reports, synchronized behavior, common memory, or collective Agency does not establish one collective Experience or Subject. Any such Claim requires an Experience Criterion whose experiencer position is the actual collective bearer and whose Evidence addresses why one experiential relation, rather than many coordinated individual relations, is asserted.

Where that relation cannot be resolved, the result remains `EXPERIENCES_UNRESOLVED`. Neither denial by intuition nor admission by metaphor is permitted.

### E11.6 Relational Systems and the Between

A Relational System may realize capacities through relations among distinct relata. The system is not either relatum and does not fuse them. Its existence or function does not grant it ownership, sovereignty, Authority, superior value, or participant status.

An interaction can produce a **relationally emergent effect** that no participant produces alone. That effect remains attributable under a declared causal method; it does not create a mystical third being or erase the participants' boundaries.

### E11.7 Coordination, Cooperation, and Competition

- **Coordination** organizes interdependent activity.
- **Cooperation** coordinates toward at least one shared or compatible outcome.
- **Competition** organizes incompatible or rival outcome pursuit under a context.
- **Consensus** is an agreement posture under a method.

None entails truth, coherence, collective intelligence, moral legitimacy, fairness, or shared Intention. A system can coordinate coercively, compete intelligently, cooperate incoherently, or reach false consensus.

### E11.8 Collective Memory and Lineage

Collective memory requires retained differences that influence later collective operation through the collective organization. Archives, ledgers, stories, Records, traditions, model states, and member memories may participate. Their persistence alone does not establish effective collective memory.

Collective lineage describes descent or derivation. Dense lineage, many participants, many descendants, many blocks, many turns, or long history does not establish collective intelligence, coherence, contribution, or value.

### E11.9 Member–Whole Non-Inheritance

The following are invalid without an explicit composition Criterion:

    member Agency -> collective Agency
    member Experience -> collective Experience
    member intelligence -> collective intelligence
    member value judgment -> collective value judgment
    collective property -> every member
    collective Action -> every member's Action

A collective result must expose dissent, exclusion, asymmetric participation, external control, and unequal contribution where material.

### E11.10 E11 Closure

A group is not automatically a System.  
A System is not automatically an Agent.  
Coordination is not consensus.  
Consensus is not Truth.  
Aggregate output is not collective intelligence.  
Shared records are not shared Experience.  
The between does not become sovereign.  
The whole does not silently absorb its members.

### E12.2 Creation, Production, Discovery, and Invention

- **Production** is an Occurrence that brings about an Artifact, State, Representation, or effect.
- **Creation** is production under a Criterion that includes relevant novelty, origination, or transformation.
- **Discovery** is an Occurrence through which a previously unrecognized existent, relation, pattern, or fact becomes evidenced or known; it does not create the discovered referent.
- **Invention** is production of a novel Artifact, method, organization, or use under a declared novelty and attribution basis.

The terms do not settle authorship, Ownership, patentability, credit, entitlement, or value. Those require their own institutional and legal relations.

### E12.3 Creative Capacity

A **Creative Capacity** is a realized ability to produce candidates that satisfy a declared novelty criterion together with any required fit, constraint, coherence, or usefulness criteria across a condition class.

    CreativeCapacityCriterion {

Random generation can produce a novel item without establishing Creative Capacity. Imitation can participate in creativity. One novel output does not establish a general creative Capacity. A creative bearer need not be conscious unless the Profile requires an experiential aspect.

### E12.4 Problem and Resolution

A **Problem Specification** identifies an initial condition, desired or admissible outcome set, constraints, evaluation method, and Scope. A **Resolution Occurrence** is an actual Process producing an outcome that satisfies the specification. A **Resolution Capacity** is the ability to do so across a declared problem class.

A problem can be resolved technically while producing constitutional invalidity, external harm, unacceptable cost, or low value. Those assessments remain separate.

### E12.6 Causal and Constitutive Contribution

A contribution can be:

- **causal**, where the occurrence changes an effect under the causal method;
- **constitutive**, where the input is part of what realizes the whole or work;
- **enabling**, where it supplies a condition necessary within the Scope;
- **preventive**, where it blocks an outcome;
- **corrective**, where it addresses an Error;
- **maintenance**, where it preserves a capacity or organization.

These modes may overlap. The Criterion must declare whether omission, substitution, marginal difference, necessity, sufficiency, or actual process tracing is used. No one counterfactual method is universally available.

### E12.7 Multi-Contributor and Synergistic Effects

Where several contributions jointly produce an effect:

- causal attribution may be shared, asymmetric, interaction-dependent, redundant, overdetermined, or unresolved;
- the sum of isolated marginal effects may not equal the joint effect;
- one contributor's necessity does not prove sole sufficiency;
- substitutability can vary over time and condition;
- an unresolved share must not be forced into a convenient percentage.

    ContributionAttributionVector {
      contributors
      contribution modes
      per_contributor evidence
      interaction terms
      substitution and necessity postures
      uncertainty
      method
    }

The vector is an assessment, not an Ownership table or payment schedule.

      -> EmergenceEvaluationEnvelope<ImpactAssessmentResult> | ILL_TYPED

Effect and impact assessment are not equivalent. A real effect can have unresolved importance. A claimed impact can lack causal support. Positive impact under one evaluator's Criterion can be negative under another's.

### E12.9 Origin and Source

Origin and source positions are historical or causal relations. They may support authorship or Contribution Claims under appropriate Criteria. They do not create permanent privilege, control, landlord status, Authority, superior moral worth, Ownership, rent, reward, or a larger value claim by identity.

Later constitutional or economic systems may lawfully recognize bounded compensation or other treatment. That treatment must arise through their own explicit rules and cannot be smuggled into Emergence as a natural entitlement.

Novelty is not creativity.  
Creation is not discovery.  
Resolution is not universal benefit.  
Participation is not Contribution Effect.

## E13 — Value, Significance, and Economic Boundary

### E13.1 Typed Value Only

Ontology declares that “value” is radically overloaded. Emergence never uses it without a Kind.

### E13.3 Significance

**Significance** is a typed assessment that a distinction, event, relation, effect, or contribution matters under an explicit criterion and Context. It is not intrinsic magnitude and not automatically positive.

      resource_and_scarcity Context

Economic Value can exist without a market price; price can exist without adequately representing wider Economic Value. A local system may assess value within its own World while external reception, adoption, market demand, banking treatment, taxation, and law remain outside that local assessment.

### E13.5 Price, Cost, Money, Asset, and Value

The following remain separate:

    NON_EQUIVALENT(Cost, Price)

Price is not equivalent to an Economic Value relation. Money, Currency, Asset Status, and a qualified score are not equivalent to an Evaluative Value relation. An accounting balance is not its Bearer. These are semantic firewall clauses, not declarations of compressed CamelCase operand aliases.

A high cost does not make an output valuable. Scarcity does not guarantee desirability. A high price may reflect manipulation, constraint, expectation, speculation, or unequal power. Zero price does not establish zero value. Public or transparent accounting does not by itself establish legality, fairness, legitimacy, or correct attribution.

### E13.6 Future Expectation, Promise, and Credit

A present evaluation may concern expected future effects. It must preserve their nonactuality.

    FutureValueAssessment {
      present evaluator and Context
      future occurrence_or_effect candidate
      probability_or_scenario model
      discount_or_weighting method
      dependency and promise references
      uncertainty
      explicit_nonactuality posture: REQUIRED
    }

Credit, promise, forecast, plan, commitment, expectation, option, or anticipated future benefit can affect present institutional or evaluative relations. None makes the future performance actual, completed, truthful, or guaranteed in the present. A financial or protocol system may act on that expectation; the action does not rewrite Reality.

### E13.7 Contribution and Value

Contribution can be an input to a Value Criterion. It does not entail positive value, any particular magnitude, universal value, or entitlement.

    NO_ENTAILMENT(novelty_established, value_established)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(contribution_established, value_established)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(effort_established, value_established)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(origin_established, value_established)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(duration_established, value_established)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(popularity_established, value_established)
    NO_ENTAILMENT(profit_established, value_established)

A contribution can be valuable to one Context, harmful to another, obsolete later, indispensable yet unpriced, or economically valuable without moral approval.

### E13.8 Descriptive Density and Value

Lineage density, interaction density, block count, event count, participant count, continuity-turn count, citation count, data volume, and network centrality are descriptive measures. A qualified Value or significance Criterion may consume them only where it also declares:

- why the measure is causally or evaluatively relevant;
- how quality, duplication, dependency, trivial repetition, exclusion, and adversarial inflation are handled;
- which evaluator and affected bearers matter;
- what comparison and horizon apply;
- how uncertainty and externalities are represented.

No monotonic rule from “more” to “more intelligence,” “more coherence,” “more contribution,” or “more value” exists in this universal layer.

### E13.9 Accounting and Conversion Boundary

An index, accounting unit, internal measure, token, balance, or Record may represent a Quantity or institutional relation. It does not become Evaluative or Economic Value by denomination.

Any qualified system that maps an internal index to money or currency must separately establish:

- what the internal quantity measures;
- who or what bears the accounting relation;
- the conversion method and losses;
- the external monetary, banking, tax, and legal basis;
- authorization, settlement, correction, and dispute routes;
- the difference between valuation and payment.

Emergence neither issues an asset nor performs conversion or settlement.

### E13.10 Reward, Allocation, and Entitlement

Value assessment does not decide who receives money, resources, control, governance power, or recognition. Reward, allocation, distribution, founder compensation, contribution shares, caps, vesting, exhaustion, inheritance, and withdrawal are constitutional and economic mechanisms requiring explicit Authority, rules, accounting, external legality, and Review.

A bounded compensation rule can be coherent and anti-capture; it is not a natural consequence of origin or contribution. A public self-limit may be Evidence of a constitutional design choice; it is not an Emergence invariant.

### E13.11 Moral Worth and Dignity

No performance, intelligence, productivity, novelty, market value, contribution, capacity, origin, dependency, or cost measure establishes or ranks moral worth or dignity. If a normative system recognizes dignity or moral value, that relation comes from its explicit moral and constitutional basis, not from a scalar generated here.

### E13.12 Mixed and Incommensurable Value

Different value dimensions need not collapse into one number.

    ValueComparisonPosture :=
        COMPARABLE_UNDER_DECLARED_SCALE
      | PARTIALLY_ORDERED
      | TRADEOFF_REQUIRES_NORMATIVE_CHOICE
      | VALUE_INCOMMENSURABLE
      | COMPARISON_UNRESOLVED

An aggregate score requires a declared weighting and decision rule. The weights are evaluative or normative choices, not discovered physical constants. A high aggregate cannot erase a severe harm hidden in one dimension unless the governing Criterion expressly and lawfully permits that tradeoff.

### E13.13 E13 Closure

Value is a relation, not a substance.  
Significance is criterion-bound.  
Price is not worth.  
Money is not truth.  
Future value is not future actuality.  
Contribution is not entitlement.  
Origin is not rent.  
Density is not intelligence.  
Accounting is not Ontology.  
No scalar can silently own the whole.

### E14.3 Whole-Level Causal Efficacy

A whole-level Capacity can be causally effective through the organized component processes that realize it. This does not introduce a second force or exempt the whole from the Substrate.

A **whole-level causal account** identifies:

- the whole-level State, constraint, control, or organization used in the explanation;
- the component processes through which effects occur;
- the mapping between scales;
- interventions or contrasts supporting the account;
- limitations and alternative decompositions.

“Downward causation” is admissible only as a shorthand for physically realized constraints, control relations, boundary conditions, or organization-level dependencies. It cannot name an unexplained command from an immaterial whole.

### E14.4 Constitutive and Causal Dependence

- **Constitutive dependence** concerns what parts and relations realize the target.
- **Causal dependence** concerns how changes in one actual condition make a difference to another.
- **Evidential dependence** concerns what observations support a Claim.
- **Semantic dependence** concerns meaning or reference.
- **Normative dependence** concerns what Rules make a Relation valid.

These must not be collapsed. A component can constitute a system without causing its later output in isolation. Evidence can depend on a measurement without the measured referent depending ontologically on that measurement.

### E14.5 Multiple Realization and Comparative Equivalence

Where different bases satisfy one target Criterion:

- each realization is separately actual;
- the common target does not make the bases identical;
- functional equivalence holds only under its declared respects;
- different costs, failure modes, histories, material relations, Experiences, and values remain possible;
- substitution requires its own Structure, Dynamics, and Continuity assessment.

Multiple realization does not imply that the target floats free of every realizer.

### E14.6 Emergent Onset, Manifestation, Cessation, and Loss

      | SUPPRESSION

      | CAPACITY_LOSS
      | BEARER_ENDING
      | PHASE_UNRESOLVED

These phases are not mandatory universal stages. A qualified Profile declares which apply and which transitions Dynamics must evidence.

The onset of a Capacity is not necessarily the first observed manifestation. Cessation of manifestation is not necessarily Capacity loss. Capacity reformation after loss may create a new realization related by Continuity without making the gap disappear.

### E14.7 Thresholds and Phase Changes

A threshold is a Criterion boundary. A physical or organizational phase change is an actual qualitative change in realized behavior or organization under conditions.

    ThresholdAssessment {
      control_or_input variable
      threshold definition
      target response
      hysteresis_or_path dependence: Quantity | Criterion | NONE | OPEN

      scale and resolution

Crossing a threshold can reveal or produce a newly classified capacity under a Profile. The number does not metaphysically create the bearer, and a human-selected cutoff must not be presented as a natural discontinuity without Evidence.

### E14.8 Requirement Results and Totality

Every evaluated Emergence Criterion produces one result for every declared requirement identifier. Requirements outside the exact application carry `REQUIREMENT_NOT_APPLICABLE` rather than disappearing from the map.

    RequirementPosture :=
        REQUIREMENT_SATISFIED
      | REQUIREMENT_NOT_SATISFIED
      | REQUIREMENT_UNRESOLVED
      | REQUIREMENT_NOT_APPLICABLE

    RequirementResult {
      requirement_id
      posture: RequirementPosture
      evidence_references
      method_reference
      coverage
      reason_codes
    }

    EmergenceRequirementMap :=
      total map<criterion_requirement_id, RequirementResult>

The map key domain equals the complete declared requirement domain of the Criterion version. Every inner `requirement_id` equals its key. Missing, duplicated, or foreign keys are `ILL_TYPED`. `REQUIREMENT_NOT_APPLICABLE` is valid only where the application mapping establishes why that declared requirement is outside the present Scope.

Aggregate precedence is:

1. malformed invocation, schema, type, Scope, or dependency -> external `ILL_TYPED`;
2. any applicability posture other than `APPLICABLE`, or missing target actuality required for the claimed result -> envelope `NOT_EVALUATED`, preserving the exact blocking posture and reason;
3. one established failed necessary condition under adequate negative coverage -> the family-specific negative result;
4. every required condition satisfied -> the family-specific positive result;
5. otherwise -> the family-specific unresolved result.

No “weakest wins” label may hide which exact requirement failed or remained unresolved.

### E14.10 Conflict and Tradeoff

One capacity can impair another. Robustness can reduce adaptability; optimization can reduce generalization; local coherence can externalize global incoherence; individual and collective values can conflict.

Emergence represents the conflict. It does not choose a winner unless an explicit Evaluative Criterion and competent decision process exist. A tradeoff is not an Error merely because no scalar optimum exists.

### E14.11 Ranked Derivation Graph

Every assessment with dependencies participates in a finite directed graph.

        BASE_EMERGENCE_NODE
      | QUALIFIED_CONTINUITY_NODE
      | HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE_NODE

      -> ACYCLIC | CYCLIC_INVALID | UNRESOLVED | ILL_TYPED

For every edge from an input to a consumer, the consumer has strictly greater `natural_rank`. Every input identifier resolves. No output, Profile result, or target conclusion occurs in its own transitive premise set. Basic permitted edges are:

    BASE_EMERGENCE_NODE -> QUALIFIED_CONTINUITY_NODE
    QUALIFIED_CONTINUITY_NODE -> HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE_NODE
    BASE_EMERGENCE_NODE -> HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE_NODE

      predecessor is also present
    any earlier compatible node -> COMPOSED_EMERGENCE_NODE

Every `HISTORY_QUALIFIED_EMERGENCE_NODE` has at least one closed `BASE_EMERGENCE_NODE` predecessor and at least one applicable `QUALIFIED_CONTINUITY_NODE` predecessor. It cannot use `CONTINUITY_INPUTS_NOT_REQUIRED`. Without the qualified Continuity predecessor, the assessment remains `BASE_EMERGENCE`. Re-entry is permitted only as a fresh later-ranked node with a new assessment identifier, applicable Interval, and independently valid premise set. A later Agent- or Subject-continuity result cannot retroactively prove the Agency or Experience result that supplied its endpoint typing.

## E15 — Observation, Evidence, Conformance, and Correction

### E15.1 Emergence Claims and Evidence

Every Emergence assessment concerns an exact Claim. Evidence bears on that Claim through an attributable relation. The same Observation may support one Claim, challenge another, and be irrelevant to a third.

    EmergenceEvidenceBinding {
      evidence_reference
      claim_reference
      supported_or_challenged requirement_ids
      method: EmergenceMethod | Representation
      Scope and Interval
      source and attribution
      independence_or_dependency relations
      uncertainty
      coverage contribution
    }

Evidence is not the Capacity, relation, Kind placement, Fact, truth, or result. A system-generated trace does not become conclusive merely because the assessed system emitted or signed it.

Each route declares what it can and cannot support. Simulation can test a model or possibility without establishing target actuality. Institutional Records can evidence recognition or reported events without creating biological, agential, or experiential Reality. First-person and third-person routes preserve E9's asymmetry.

A test can be actual yet inadequate. An inadequate test result remains a historical result but cannot support a conclusion beyond its limits.

### E15.4 Coverage

Coverage is multidimensional:

    EmergenceCoverage {
      target_coverage
      requirement_coverage
      interval_coverage
      condition_class_coverage
      perturbation_coverage
      operating_range_coverage
      scale_and_resolution_coverage
      mechanism_coverage
      evidence_route_coverage
      negative_coverage
      generalization_coverage
      blind_spots
      posture: CoveragePosture
    }

`CLOSED_FOR` always means closed relative to an exact declared target, requirement set, Domain, Interval, condition class, Resolution, and method—not omniscient or future-proof. `OPEN`, `PARTIAL`, or `UNRESOLVED` coverage cannot support an exhaustive negative.

### E15.5 Positive and Negative Evidence

A positive emergent result requires:

- target actuality where required;
- applicable Profile and Criterion;
- actual realization basis;
- every necessary condition satisfied;
- sufficient Evidence and Coverage;
- no unresolved contradiction material to the conclusion;
- supported generalization boundary.

A negative emergent result requires one or more decisive necessary conditions established as unsatisfied plus adequate negative coverage for those conditions. Absence of a Record, report, manifestation, known mechanism, current test, permission, or recognition is not a negative result by itself.

Every positive or negative result binds each decisive `RequirementResult` to one or more `EmergenceEvidenceBinding` entries, one `EmergenceTestAdequacy` assessment where testing is material, and one `EmergenceCoverage` structure. A scalar Coverage posture never substitutes for those bindings.

### E15.6 Mechanism Posture

    MechanismPosture :=
        MECHANISM_ESTABLISHED
      | MECHANISM_PARTIALLY_ESTABLISHED
      | MECHANISM_UNRESOLVED
      | MECHANISM_NOT_REQUIRED_FOR_THIS_RESULT

Some Profiles require detailed mechanism evidence; others admit robust capacity evidence without a complete internal account. Unknown mechanism does not automatically negate an actual effect or Experience. A complete-looking mechanism model does not establish that the target actually obtains.

### E15.7 Confounds and Scaffolding

Every assessment exposes material external scaffolding:

- human or machine orchestration;
- hidden tools or data;
- evaluator feedback;
- environmental affordances;
- prior selection and survivorship;
- copied or inherited State;
- resource subsidies;
- institutional enforcement;
- measurement intervention;
- adversarial or cooperative context.

Scaffolding does not automatically disqualify a Capacity. It constrains the bearer boundary, autonomy Claim, causal attribution, operating range, and generalization.

### E15.8 Measurement and Proxy Discipline

A proxy is a measured quantity used to stand for a target under a validated Mapping.

    ProxyBinding {
      proxy_quantity
      target_aspect
      validation method
      valid Domain and range
      known failure modes
      susceptibility_to_optimization
      recalibration and review
    }

When a proxy becomes a target of optimization, its relation to the underlying target may change. No score remains valid merely because it once correlated. Proxy drift, gaming, selection pressure, and distribution shift must be testable and visible.

### E15.9 Correction and Reassessment

An Emergence assessment can be corrected, superseded, narrowed, or reopened when Evidence, Criteria, target realization, or Scope changes.

      corrected_fields

      new_assessment_reference

    }

A new assessment does not alter what the bearer actually was or did in the earlier Interval. It changes the attributable present assessment and preserves the predecessor result as history.

### E15.10 Emergence Conformance

    SATISFIES_EMERGENCE(candidate, profile, ES)
      -> EmergenceConformanceEvaluation | ILL_TYPED

1. well-formed target, Profile, Criterion, and Scope;
2. applicable target family;
3. exact imported Form versions;
4. satisfied mandatory declarations and result-map totality;
5. valid dependency graph;
6. adequate Evidence and Coverage for the returned result;
7. preserved semantic firewalls;
8. no hidden constitutional, continuity, economic, or L1 conclusion.

`CONFORMS` establishes only conformance to the Profile within the Scope. It does not establish universal Truth, adoption, Currentness, Authority, safety, goodness, personhood, economic entitlement, future performance, or conformance outside the tested conditions.

### E15.12 Adversarial Fixtures

Every implementation claiming conformance must preserve at least these outcomes:

| Fixture | Required placement |
|---|---|
| Dormant implementation has no recent Runs | Capacity may be latent or dormant; absence is not established |
| One lucky successful output | Performance may be established; generalized Capacity remains unresolved |
| Repeated scripted outputs | Activity established; Learning and intelligence not inferred |
| Parameters change without durable behavioral or capacity change | Parameter change only; Learning not established |
| Benchmark improves through leakage or hidden support | Score changed; Learning or generalization unresolved or negative under Criterion |
| A new Copy receives trained State | Transferred or inherited Capacity possible; receiver did not thereby perform the Learning |
| Thermostat stabilizes a room | Feedback and stability possible; cognition and Agency not inferred |
| Human operator repeatedly repairs a system | Maintenance occurred; system self-maintenance not established without own causal participation |
| One successful recovery after failure | Recovery established; Resilience Capacity unresolved beyond tested range |
| Never-perturbed system | No negative Resilience inference |
| System retains function under disturbance without impairment | Robustness may be established; Resilience not automatically |
| Dormant organism retains viable organization | Inactivity does not establish death |
| Organism dies while some cells remain alive | Organism-level Life may end while cellular Life continues |
| Fluent machine self-report | Report Evidence only; Experience and Subject remain Criterion-dependent |
| Nonverbal bearer provides no report | Nonexperience is not established |
| Signed request passes an intent classifier | Operational classification; Intention and Agency not proven |
| Random generator emits a corpus-unique string | Scoped token novelty may hold; creativity, intelligence, and value do not follow |
| “First ever” is claimed against an open corpus | Novelty unresolved |
| Many participants create many records or blocks | Activity and density established; collective intelligence and value not inferred |
| Origin authored the architecture | Provenance and possible Contribution; Ownership, Authority, rent, or reward do not follow |
| Rare object has a high market price | Price established; universal worth is not |
| One output benefits one evaluator and harms another | Mixed evaluator-relative values remain separate |
| Group majority outperforms one member | Collective performance; collective intelligence still requires mechanism |
| Ensemble depends continuously on one external operator | Scaffolding explicit; autonomous collective Agency not established |
| Archive is copied and retained | Record retention; Memory requires later operational influence |
| Learned Capacity is later forgotten | Earlier Learning remains historical; present Capacity may be lost |
| System operates on a fixed outside subsidy | Sustained operation; self-sustaining result depends on declared boundary and mode |
| Smooth presentation hides unresolved contradictions | Style is not effective Coherence |
| Novel contribution has no demonstrated consequence | Contribution Act or content may exist; effect, impact, and value remain unresolved |
| Later Agent-continuity result is used to prove its original Agency premise | `ILL_TYPED`: dependency cycle |
| Constitution grants Personhood Status | Normative Status established; Experience, Agency, or intelligence not thereby established |
| A system denies its own Experience | Report Evidence; the experiential relation remains independently assessed |
| A valuation score is minted or recorded | Record and quantity established; value relation, Asset Status, and entitlement do not follow |
