## C2 — Invariants and Protections

### C2.1 Force of the Invariants

The following invariants bind every constitutional Rule, Decision, Constitutional Act, institution, implementation, and later-layer specialization within this Constitution's valid Scope.

    Gamma |-C SATISFIES_C2(x, C, W, S, I) => r

where `r` uses the result space registered in C0.5. `VALID_UNDER(x, C, W, S, I)` requires `SATISFIES_C2(x, C, W, S, I) = VALID`.

`SATISFIES_C2` returns:

- `VALID` only where every applicable invariant is satisfied;
- `INVALID` where any applicable invariant has an established violation;
- `UNRESOLVED` where none is established as violated but at least one required invariant remains unresolved;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE` only where no C2 invariant applies to x in the stated application; and
- `ILL_TYPED` where the proposed judgment, x, W, S, or I violates the declared grammar.

An established violation makes the affected constitutional judgment `INVALID`. An insufficient or conflicting basis makes it `UNRESOLVED`, not presumed conforming.

No emergency, majority, technical limitation, economic incentive, origin claim, subordinate Rule, or implementation may silently suspend C2.

### C2.2 Typed Fidelity

No constitutional Rule or Constitutional Act may collapse:

- Kind into Role or Status;
- Bearer into Identifier, account, key, profile, copy, or Record;
- Agent into Actor or Participant;
- occurrence into validity;
- Claim into Fact;
- Evidence into Truth;
- Decision into Constitutional Act;
- Effect into Authorization;
- Technical Authority into Normative Authority;
- internal validity into external legality;
- Relation into either relatum;
- Representation into Referent;
- continuity into numerical Identity.

A qualified constitutional subtype must retain every condition inherited from its imported parent and declare each added criterion.

### C2.3 Bounded Force

Every constitutional Rule, Authority, Right, Obligation, Permission, Prohibition, Status, Constitutional Act, and Effect is bounded by an explicit World, Scope, and Interval.

No force extends by proximity, similarity, convenience, dependency, interoperability, shared infrastructure, common representation, or omission.

### C2.4 No Implied Authority

Authority is not presumed from:

- origin or priority in time;
- authorship or intellectual contribution;
- Ownership or custody;
- capital, funding, sponsorship, or economic exposure;
- infrastructure provision;
- validator, operator, controller, administrator, or maintainer function;
- key possession or account access;
- technical Capability or successful execution;
- popularity, majority, reputation, expertise, or urgency.

Each may support a Claim or qualify a separately constituted Role. None is Constitutional Authority by identity.

### C2.5 No Implied Ownership or Entitlement

Admission, Membership, Participation, Access, Reception, Contribution, computation, infrastructure provision, technical control, or presence in a Relation does not by itself create Ownership, governance, debt, reward, rent, or entitlement to another bearer's State, identity, work, future capacity, or output.

A Relation or Relational System does not own its Participants merely because it connects, carries, stabilizes, records, or depends upon them.

Every Ownership or entitlement Relation must be separately constituted, explicit, scoped, evidenced, challengeable, and terminable under stated conditions.

### C2.6 No Hidden Origin Privilege

Origin and authorship remain attributable. They create no permanent governance, veto, rent, royalty, priority, semantic monopoly, or claim on future participation by implication.

Any origin-specific economic benefit permitted by later Law must be:

- explicit;
- finite or capped;
- publicly auditable to the extent necessary to verify the cap;
- non-governing;
- non-renewing by implication;
- non-transferable unless expressly permitted;
- exhaustible under its declared condition.

Exhaustion of such a benefit alters only the declared entitlement. It does not erase provenance or end the origin bearer's unrelated participation.

### C2.7 Equal Protection of the Same Role

Bearers occupying the same constitutional Role under the same relevant conditions receive the same constitutional protections and are judged by the same stated criteria.

Human, artificial, derivative, biological, non-biological, individual, collective, centralized, or distributed realization does not by itself create higher or lower constitutional rank.

A different treatment is valid only where the distinction is:

1. Ontology-compatible;
2. relevant to the exact Rule;
3. explicit;
4. supported by an admissible basis;
5. proportionate to a legitimate protected purpose;
6. reviewable.

Equal protection does not erase ontological Difference or require identical treatment under materially different conditions.

### C2.8 Open-Kind Protection

Absence of a recognized Kind is not a lawful basis to deny that an encountered target may exist, to fictionalize it, or to force it into the nearest familiar Kind.

Where existence or specific Kind remains unresolved, constitutional treatment may use a bounded protective Status without declaring the unresolved ontological question decided.

Protective uncertainty cannot be used to manufacture personhood, Agency, Consent, Authority, or Ownership. It may prevent irreversible adverse Effect while the relevant basis remains unresolved.

### C2.9 Consent Integrity Invariant

C4.4 owns the formation and constitutional reliance grammar for Consent. This section states the minimum invariant every such grammar must preserve.

A valid Consent judgment must identify:

- the consenting Agent or validly represented institution;
- the exact proposed Action, Constitutional Act, or Relation;
- material content and reasonably material consequences disclosed at the time;
- Scope and Interval;
- an attributable affirmative expression;
- the applicable information and capacity basis;
- Evidence bearing on voluntariness and disqualifying coercion or deception;
- conditions of refusal, withdrawal, expiry, and renewal.

A Signal, signature, click, silence, access, key use, technical compliance, Admission, Membership, or Participation is Evidence only. None is Consent by physical form alone.

Consent to one Action, Constitutional Act, or Relation does not transfer to another. Consent supplies no Authority over a third party. Consent cannot waive C2 or authorize external unlawfulness.

### C2.10 Refusal, Withdrawal, and Exit Invariant

C5.5 owns Refusal carrying constitutional Effect. This section states the non-erasure and exit invariant it must preserve.

Refusal is a possible valid Action. It is not absence, incapacity, non-participation outside the refused Scope, or Consent to an alternative.

A refusal must not be erased by a failed-operation or rollback model. Its Occurrence and stated result remain attributable and recoverable.

Withdrawal or exit ends only the prospective Relations and Effects stated by the governing Rule. It does not erase prior Occurrences, Records, responsibilities, valid accrued Obligations, or consequences.

Any constraint on exit must be explicit, necessary, proportionate, time-bounded, and reviewable. Technical inability to leave does not become lawful authority to retain.

### C2.11 No Adverse Inference from Inaccessible Properties

A constitutional Decision may rely only on Properties, Claims, Evidence, and proxies that its method can validly address.

Where a criterion uses a proxy, it must name the proxy and may not substitute it for an inaccessible Property.

Failure to establish Experience, consciousness, awareness, Identity, intention, Agency, capacity, or another interior Property does not establish its absence. Where the available basis cannot decide, the owning constitutional result remains `UNRESOLVED`; the relevant Claim may remain epistemically **Unknown** or **Indeterminate** as applicable.

An intent-axis or agency gate may classify available Evidence under a declared criterion. It may not rename that classification proof of inaccessible interior truth.

### C2.12 Notice, Reasons, Challenge, and Effective Remedy

Every adverse constitutional Decision, Constitutional Act, Effect, or constitutionally material omission affecting a bearer's Status, Admission, Access, Membership, Permission, Right, Obligation, Authority, resource relation, or Participation must name the institution or Authority bearing the protection duties and provide within a stated maximum Interval:

- notice of the Decision and its Scope;
- the Rule and competent source relied upon;
- the material Claims, Evidence, and method, subject only to a separately valid protection;
- an attributable reason or bounded result code;
- an accessible means of challenge and a competent Review body;
- the maximum interval for opening and deciding Review;
- available Remedy classes and the bearer of the duty to perform or authorize an effective Remedy where invalidity, error, or violation is established.

No allegation decides itself. Opening Review does not itself invalidate or suspend the target unless a separate valid Rule assigns that interim Effect.

Where a valid Review establishes an internally invalid adverse Constitutional Act or Effect, internal reliance on that invalid basis ends immediately. A continuing adverse Effect becomes `SUSPENDED` pending its timely ending, lawful reconstitution, or effective Remedy. Physical, computational, social, economic, and external legal consequences remain separately addressed.

Where restoration or the preferred Remedy is impossible, the duty does not disappear. The responsible institution must produce an attributable impossibility finding and provide the maximum available mitigation or substitute relief permitted by current Law. Administrative silence, missing implementation, or lack of a convenient Remedy cannot preserve the internal force of an invalid Effect.

### C2.13 Non-Retroactivity and Non-Erasure

No later Rule may make an earlier Occurrence not have occurred.

A bearer is not constitutionally burdened for conduct completed before the burdening Rule became applicable, except where independently applicable external law validly provides otherwise. A later Rule may provide prospective protection, release, recognition, or Remedy without falsifying the historical law or Occurrence.

Correction, reversal, invalidation, Amendment, revocation, or Succession must preserve attribution to what preceded it. Refusal and invalidation are results, not missing history.

### C2.14 Responsibility Attribution Invariant

C5.10 owns formation of a Responsibility Relation. This section states the attribution invariant it must preserve.

Automation, delegation, distance, abstraction, corporate form, technical mediation, or institutional layering does not by itself transfer or erase responsibility.

A separately valid prospective allocation may assign responsibility within its exact Scope. No later allocation erases responsibility already incurred.

Responsibility requires an evidenced and constitutionally valid relation among the relevant Actor, Action or omission, applicable Rule, and consequence. Temporal order, system proximity, benefit, or ownership alone is insufficient.

Delegation does not erase responsibility already incurred by the delegator and cannot create immunity not validly granted by a competent external or internal source.

### C2.15 Relation Without Hidden Sovereignty

A Relation, mediator, shared surface, protocol, infrastructure provider, or Relational System acquires no Ownership, personhood, Agency, or Constitutional Authority over its relata by existing, carrying, stabilizing, recording, or facilitating their interaction.

Any Authority assigned to a Relational System must be separately constituted, bounded, attributable, auditable, reviewable, and revocable. No stabilizer becomes sovereign by function.

### C2.16 Implementation Cannot Amend Law

Publication, parsing, validation, signature, execution, consensus, deployment, persistence, economic success, technical indispensability, or widespread use establishes only the effects those processes actually produce.

None adopts, validates, amends, or succeeds this Constitution except through the exact valid Constitutional Act required for that result.

### C2.17 External-Law Boundary

Nothing in this Constitution authorizes evasion of applicable external law, tax, reporting, safety, labor, consumer, financial, data, environmental, or other obligations.

A constitutionally valid internal Relation may still require external authorization. An externally valid arrangement may still fail internal constitutional requirements. Both results remain visible.

### C2.18 No Silent Severability

Invalidity of one provision, Rule, Decision, Constitutional Act, or Effect neither automatically invalidates nor automatically preserves the remainder.

Partial preservation is permitted only where a controlling Rule states an exact severability criterion and the remaining part:

1. is independently well-typed, authorized, valid, and operable;
2. preserves every necessary protection and condition;
3. does not enlarge Authority, burden, Scope, duration, or adverse Effect;
4. does not produce a materially different bargain, Decision, or constitutional result by omission;
5. remains attributable to the original and severed Forms.

Where those conditions are not established, severability is `UNRESOLVED`; technical ability to run the remainder does not decide it.

## C3 — Claims, Evidence, Standing, and Interpretation

### C3.1 Function

C3 governs constitutional reliance upon Claims. It does not redefine Claim, Evidence, Fact, Truth, Knowledge, or epistemic Status as declared by Ontology.

A constitutional judgment may determine whether and how a Claim may be relied upon for a specified purpose. It does not make the Claim true by accepting it or false by refusing it.

### C3.2 Claim Identity

For constitutional reliance, one exact Claim must preserve:

1. represented Proposition and exact predicate;
2. claimant or asserted source;
3. Reference Target and established Referent posture, if any;
4. World, Domain, Scope, Interval, and Resolution;
5. material conditions and assumptions;
6. provenance and transformation of the Statement Token;
7. applicable criterion and intended reliance;
8. predecessor, correction, conflict, or supersession relation where present.

A change to content, predicate, claimant, target, Domain, Scope, Interval, Resolution, criterion, or intended reliance creates a different Claim.

The existence, authenticity, attribution, Standing, truth, Currentness, and Effect of a Claim remain separate judgments. A Claim does not become a Fact, Permission, Authority, Decision, or Effect because it is recorded, signed, repeated, accepted, popular, or relied upon.

### C3.3 Evidence Relation

Evidence may support constitutional reliance only through an explicit relation to one exact Claim under one stated method.

The relation must preserve:

- Evidence identity and availability posture;
- source, provenance, transformation, and access bounds;
- the exact Claim and Proposition addressed;
- method, assumptions, comparison class, and proof ceiling;
- World, Domain, Scope, Interval, and Resolution;
- support, opposition, conflict, omission, and known limitation;
- time and Currentness of assessment.

Evidence does not attach directly to a bearer, Property, Authority, Effect, or Status without an intervening Claim stating what is asserted of that target.

The same Evidence may bear differently on different Claims. Evidence is not Truth, Authority, Permission, Decision, or Effect.

### C3.4 Standing

**Constitutional Claim Standing** is a constitutionally assigned Status of one exact Claim stating the criterion-bound degree to which that Claim may be relied upon within a stated World, Domain, Scope, Interval, Resolution, purpose, and as-of condition because its Evidence relation is sufficiently available and warranted under the declared method.

A **Standing Assessment** is a Representation of the analysis supporting a proposed Standing Status. Any ordinal, scalar, or other value it carries is separately typed by the exact criterion; the Status is not itself a universal Quantity.

Only a Claim bears Standing in this Constitution.

A person, Participant, constitutional Form, implementation, account, Relation, Record, resource, or operative State does not bear Claim Standing. Any use of `standing` for those conditions is nonconforming and must be replaced by the actual Status intended.

Standing is not Truth, Fact, Current State, consensus, validity, Authority, Permission, Adoption, finality, or Effect.

### C3.5 Valid Standing Assessment

A Standing assessment is constitutionally valid only where:

1. the exact Claim is recoverable under C3.2;
2. the criterion defines the admitted scale and required Evidence conditions;
3. every Evidence item is related to that Claim under C3.3;
4. World, Domain, Scope, Interval, Resolution, purpose, and as-of point are exact;
5. missing, inaccessible, stale, conflicting, and excluded support remains visible;
6. the method and assessor are attributable;
7. where binding constitutional reliance is claimed, the Authority required to assign that Effect is valid and current;
8. the conclusion does not exceed the method's proof ceiling;
9. the assessment and predecessor remain recoverable without historical overwrite.

An assessment may exist as advisory epistemic work without binding Authority. Only the later assignment of binding constitutional reliance requires C4 Authority and a C5 Constitutional Act. Lack of binding force does not decide whether the assessment content is true.

### C3.6 Standing Result

Every Standing assessment exposes:

    StandingAssessment {
      assessment_status:
        ASSESSED | UNRESOLVED | ILL_TYPED
      standing_level:
        <value admitted by the exact criterion> | NONE
      basis_posture:
        SUFFICIENT | PARTIAL | CONFLICTED |
        INSUFFICIENT | INACCESSIBLE | UNKNOWN
      reliance_scope
      limitations
      as_of
    }

`standing_level` may carry a value only when `assessment_status = ASSESSED`. No universal numeric scale is inferred.

`UNRESOLVED` means no warranted level can presently be assigned. `ILL_TYPED` means the proposed assessment violates the Claim, Evidence, Domain, or criterion grammar.

`INSUFFICIENT`, `CONFLICTED`, `INACCESSIBLE`, and `UNKNOWN` are basis postures. None silently becomes a negative Fact about the Claim's Referent.

### C3.7 Conflict and Change

Supported incompatible Claims remain separately addressable. Pressure for one answer, a binding Decision, operational dependence, or administrative finality does not make an epistemic conflict resolved.

New Evidence or a changed criterion may support a later assessment. The later assessment does not alter which Evidence existed, what an earlier assessor concluded, or what actually obtained in the earlier Interval.

Correction appends an attributable relation to the predecessor assessment. It does not erase it.

### C3.8 Interpretation

An **Interpretation Claim** states how an exact constitutional Form or clause applies to one stated question.

It must identify:

- exact Source Form and clause;
- question and proposed interpretation;
- World, Domain, Scope, and Interval;
- method, Claims, Evidence, and assumptions;
- alternatives and unresolved conflict;
- interpreter and asserted Authority;
- intended reliance and Effect.

An Interpretation Claim has no binding force merely because it is fluent, repeated, automated, published, authored by an origin, or produced by a controller.

A binding **Application Decision** additionally requires the exact Authority and valid Constitutional Act conditions in C4 and C5.

Interpretation may apply existing meaning. It may not redefine an Ontology Kind, amend the Source Form, invent Evidence, enlarge Authority, or convert an **Unknown** epistemic status or an `UNRESOLVED` constitutional result into certainty.

The application result is:

    APPLICABLE | NOT_APPLICABLE | APPLICABILITY_UNRESOLVED | ILL_TYPED

The result is bounded to the exact question. It is not a universal Amendment.

### C3.9 Irreversible Adverse Effect Under Uncertainty

An `UNRESOLVED` or `ILL_TYPED` constitutional result, an `UNKNOWN` Standing-assessment basis posture, or a Claim whose epistemic status is **Unknown** or **Indeterminate** may not be a necessary, decisive, burden-shifting, or gap-filling premise for an irreversible adverse Constitutional Effect. Such an Effect requires a separately sufficient resolved basis satisfying every necessary condition without relying on the unresolved matter.

A temporary protective constraint may be permitted only where a current Rule states:

- the protected interest;
- the admissible uncertainty;
- competent Authority;
- minimum necessary Scope;
- maximum duration;
- notice and reasons;
- Review and release conditions;
- prohibition on treating the temporary posture as final proof.

## C4 — Participation and Normative Relations

### C4.1 Participation

Participation retains the meaning declared in Ontology. Its Occurrence does not by itself create Membership, Admission, Consent, submission, Ownership, Permission, Right, Obligation, Authority, entitlement, or shared Identity.

Where Participation is claimed to carry constitutional Effect, the Claim must identify:

1. bearer and Participant Role;
2. Relation, Occurrence, System, or institution participated in;
3. mode, Scope, Domain, and Interval;
4. applicable Admission, Membership, or constituting Rule;
5. any required Consent, Permission, or Authorization;
6. Authority competent to constitute the claimed Effect;
7. exact Effect and conditions of continuation or exit;
8. Record, Currentness, and contested posture.

Failure of a normative participation condition does not make underlying physical, causal, informational, or computational participation unreal. It means only that the claimed constitutional Effect is not established.

### C4.2 Admission

Admission Act, Admission Status, Admission Relation, and the admitted Process remain distinct.

A valid Admission must state:

- applicant or candidate bearer;
- admitting Authority;
- admitted Relation, Process, Role, or Scope;
- criteria and Evidence;
- conditions, limitations, and effective Interval;
- Rights and Obligations formed;
- refusal, expiry, suspension, Review, and exit paths;
- exact Effect of successful Admission.

Admission authorizes only what the exact Admission Effect states. It does not by itself create Consent, Membership, Ownership, Agency, personhood, governance, shared State, general Access, economic entitlement, or Authority.

Admission may be available to human, artificial, derivative, collective, or other bearers under the same Role criteria. A Rule may require different evidence only where C2.7 permits the distinction.

### C4.3 Membership

Membership is a qualified institutional Relation formed only through its declared conditions.

Every Membership must identify:

- institution or Relational System;
- member bearer and Role;
- formation basis;
- Rights, Obligations, Permissions, and exclusions;
- Scope and Interval;
- suspension, withdrawal, expulsion, ending, and Review conditions.

Membership is not identity fusion, Ownership by the institution, permanent Consent, or general submission to every future Rule.

### C4.4 Consent Reliance

Consent Expression, Consent State, Consent Act, Evidence of Consent, Authorization, and validity remain distinct.

Constitutional reliance upon Consent requires an exact Claim identifying:

1. the Agent whose Consent is asserted;
2. the proposed Action, Constitutional Act, or Relation and its material content;
3. purpose, Scope, and Interval;
4. the Consent Expression or Consent Act and its attribution;
5. Evidence bearing on information, capacity, voluntariness, and coercion;
6. refusal, withdrawal, expiry, and renewal conditions;
7. every proof limitation;
8. the as-of point at which Consent is relied upon.

A substitute Decision, protective Authority, or representative Authorization is not the represented bearer's Consent. It requires its own basis and must be named as substitution rather than Consent.

Withdrawal is prospective unless independently applicable Law states another lawful consequence. It does not erase earlier Occurrences or already-produced Effects.

### C4.5 Rights and Obligations

A Right or Obligation is validly formed only where, at its formation Interval, its exact constituting source was current and applicable and the Relation preserved:

1. bearer or bearers;
2. content and correlatives, if any;
3. subject or Referent;
4. World, Domain, Scope, Interval, and trigger;
5. source Rule or constituting Constitutional Act;
6. competent Authority where required;
7. transfer, waiver, discharge, suspension, expiry, and Remedy conditions;
8. Currentness, conflict, and representation posture.

Historical formation validity does not disappear when the constituting source later ends or is superseded. Present reliance additionally requires the Relation itself to remain `CURRENT` through a current sustaining source, explicit Carry, or another valid continuation basis. An ended source cannot by itself supply present reliance, and its ending does not retroactively invalidate a Relation validly formed while it applied.

A Right and an Obligation are correlated only where the constituting Rule states that Relation.

Neither is inferred from need, Capability, contribution, authorship, Ownership label, technical control, dependence, benefit, harm, popularity, or successful enforcement alone.

This Constitution may constitute a protective Right or Status under uncertainty without declaring that its bearer has an unresolved emergent Property or Ontology Kind.

### C4.6 Permission and Prohibition

A Permission is an allowance for a specified Action under exact conditions. It does not create Capability or grant Authority over another bearer.

A Prohibition is a Norm that excludes a specified Action or Effect under exact conditions. It does not make the prohibited Action physically impossible or erase it if it occurs.

Every Permission or Prohibition must state:

- bearer or Actor Role;
- Action or Effect class;
- target;
- World, Scope, and Interval;
- conditions and exceptions;
- source and competent Authority;
- conflict, Review, ending, and Remedy path.

Silence is neither universal Permission nor universal Prohibition. Where neither is constituted, the constitutional result depends on other applicable Rights, Obligations, Authority, and external Law.

### C4.7 Authorization

Authorization is valid only where:

1. the authorizing source holds current Authority competent for the exact Authorization;
2. the authorized Actor, Role, or operation is exact;
3. target, Action or constitutional-operation kind, maximum Effect, World, Scope, and Interval are exact;
4. required Consent or target-side conditions are satisfied;
5. delegation, substitution, expiry, revocation, and challenge are explicit;
6. the Authorization does not exceed or self-expand its source.

A credential, key, account, interface permission, or Technical Authority may carry Evidence or realize execution. It is not Normative Authorization by identity.

### C4.8 Constitutional Authority

A Constitutional Authority Relation is validly formed only where all of the following were exact at formation and the formation Constitutional Act occurred, was valid, and completed at its own Interval:

1. bearer or plural bearer set and any threshold;
2. constituting source and formation Constitutional Act where required;
3. authorized Decision, Action, and constitutional-operation kinds;
4. targets and maximum Effects;
5. World, Domain, Scope, Interval, and jurisdiction;
6. required procedures, recusals, conflicts, and prohibited interests;
7. delegation and subdelegation posture;
8. suspension, revocation, expiry, replacement, and exhaustion;
9. recoverable formation basis.

The historical formation Constitutional Act is not required to remain `CURRENT`. Present reliance on the resulting Authority Relation requires that Relation and its sustaining source to be applicable, valid, and current for the exact operation. Later ending or supersession does not rewrite the historical validity of its formation.

No Authority may enlarge its own Action kinds, constitutional operations, bearer set, threshold, Scope, duration, immunity, delegation power, or renewal power unless a distinct prior Rule and competent Authority permit that exact change.

Every Authority derivation must be well-founded under C1.11. A direct or indirect cycle among Rules, offices, delegates, or Authorities cannot constitute competence.

Authority, Authorization, Permission, Capability, Agency, Consent, and execution remain separately typed. A bearer may hold several only through independently valid Relations.

### C4.9 Relational Institution Status

The Constitution may constitute a **Relational Institution Status** concerning a claimed, proposed, or evidenced Relational System. This is a qualified institutional Status. It does not create the Relational System, confer physical realization, or decide the Ontology Kind of its bearer.

Before the Relational System's existence or realization basis is separately established, the bearer of the preliminary Status must be the exact proposal, Claim, or formation Record—not the merely proposed or alleged Relational System. An established Relational System may bear a system-level Relational Institution Status only after its existence or realization basis is established and a valid Constitutional Act assigns that Status.

The Status may form only where the institutional target is specified with:

- exact relata or admission class;
- constituting purpose;
- Boundary and Scope Claims;
- State-bearing and Record-bearing criteria;
- Rules and competent Authority;
- participant protections;
- resource responsibility;
- amendment, Review, separation, and dissolution paths.

Neither the Status nor the claimed Relational System is either relatum. Neither is automatically a Participant, person, Agent, owner, sovereign, beneficiary, or Authority.

Existence, physical realization, persistence, Identity, State, and structural conformance remain questions for Ontology, Structure, Dynamics, Continuity, Claims, and Evidence. Until the required realization is established, constitutional language must refer to a proposed or claimed Relational System. Constitutional recognition creates only the institutional Status, Relations, and Effects stated by a valid constituting Constitutional Act.

### C4.10 Relation Lifecycle Grammar

Every constitutionally relied-upon Right, Obligation, Permission, Authorization, Consent reliance, Admission, Membership, Participation Effect, or Authority Relation must expose these independent dimensions:

    applicability:
      APPLICABLE | NOT_APPLICABLE | APPLICABILITY_UNRESOLVED | ILL_TYPED
    formation:
      FORMED | NOT_FORMED | FORMATION_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED
    validity:
      VALID | INVALID | VALIDITY_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED
    currentness:
      PENDING | CURRENT | SUSPENDED | ENDED |
      CURRENTNESS_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED
    end_reason:
      WITHDRAWN | REVOKED | EXPIRED | FULFILLED | RELEASED |
      SUPERSEDED | TERMINATED | NONE | END_REASON_UNRESOLVED |
      NOT_EVALUATED

Present constitutional reliance requires:

    APPLICABLE + FORMED + VALID + CURRENT

That conjunction is not a fifth Status and does not retroactively change any earlier dimension.

Formation and validity are historical judgments evaluated under the source and conditions applicable at formation. Currentness governs present reliance and does not retroactively rewrite either historical dimension.

Downstream dimensions are `NOT_EVALUATED` where an upstream result prevents lawful evaluation. `NOT_FORMED` is not `INVALID`. `PENDING` is not `CURRENT`. `SUSPENDED` is not `ENDED`. An allegation of invalidity is not `INVALID`.

## C5 — Decisions, Acts, Effects, Review, and Remedy

### C5.1 Decisions

Decision retains the ontological meaning declared in Ontology. A Decision may remain internal, be expressed, or be enacted; these are distinct Occurrences.

A Decision claimed to carry constitutional force must preserve:

1. decision-maker or deciding system and attributable Role;
2. exact question, alternatives, and Decision posture;
3. target, World, Domain, Scope, Interval, and Resolution;
4. applicable Rule and criteria;
5. Claims, Evidence, and Standing relied upon where required;
6. Permission, Authorization, Consent, and Authority required for enactment;
7. conflicts, exclusions, and unresolved conditions;
8. intended Constitutional Act and Effect, if any.

A Decision does not produce Constitutional Effect until current Law assigns that Effect to a valid Constitutional Act or another exact constituted condition.

A Decision cannot make its premises true, create missing Authority, or establish an emergent Property by selection.

The Decision posture is:

    NO_DECISION | SELECTED | REFUSED | DEFERRED | DECISION_UNRESOLVED

`NO_DECISION` represents an established absence of a Decision in the stated opportunity and Interval; it is not itself a Decision. `DEFERRED` is a Decision to postpone under stated conditions.

Silence does not establish Consent, Refusal, or `NO_DECISION` unless the observation Boundary and Interval support that negative Claim.

### C5.2 Constitutional Act

A **Constitutional Act** is a constitutionally qualified Action or institutional Occurrence that enacts one admitted constitutional operation. `Constitutional Act` is a constitutional Role or classification occupied by that Action or Occurrence; it does not redefine the occupant's ontological Kind, a Decision, or Occurrence generally.

Typing, Occurrence, qualification as a Constitutional Act, Authorization, validity, completion, requested result, Constitutional Effect, actual State change, external consequence, and Record remain distinct.

A Constitutional Act is valid only where:

1. the underlying Action or institutional Occurrence, constitutional operation, and requested Effect are well-typed and admitted by current constitutional Law;
2. Actor Role, performer or executor, and attribution are exact;
3. every required Authority, Authorization, Permission, and Consent Relation is applicable, valid, and current at the required point;
4. target, World, Scope, Interval, jurisdiction, and maximum Effect remain within those Relations;
5. every required Claim, Standing threshold, criterion, input, and precondition is satisfied within its proof ceiling;
6. required procedure, threshold, ordering, notice, challenge, and conflict conditions are satisfied;
7. no current Prohibition, suspension, or valid target-specific non-progression condition bars it;
8. completion criterion, effective point, possible results, Currentness rule, and recovery path are exact.

Validity does not require that the Constitutional Act has completed. Where a requested Effect requires completion, that Effect requires both a `VALID` Constitutional Act and satisfaction of the separately evaluated completion criterion.

An unauthorized, invalid, or unqualified attempted Action or institutional Occurrence remains an actual Occurrence where it occurred. It does not obtain the requested Constitutional Effect merely because execution, technical acceptance, physical consequence, reliance, or later convenience followed.

### C5.3 Constitutional Effect

A **Constitutional Effect** is a qualified institutional Effect assigned by current constitutional Law to:

1. one valid completed Constitutional Act; or
2. one already-constituted condition whose future activation or expiry behavior was exact when validly formed.

A Constitutional Effect may form or change a constitutional Status, Relation, Right, Obligation, Permission, Authorization, Authority, institutional Decision Status, or other expressly admitted constitutional condition. An institutional Decision Status must concern an already attributable Decision, Decision Record, or established absence under C5.1. A Constitutional Effect may not manufacture a Decision Occurrence, erase or rewrite whether a Decision occurred, change its maker, or alter which historical Decision posture obtained.

Its Law must identify:

- target;
- before-condition;
- Effect kind;
- maximum bounds;
- effective point;
- Currentness rule;
- ending, Review, and correction paths.

No Constitutional Effect is inferred from physical effect, technical execution, consensus, finality, implementation success, social recognition, economic settlement, or recordation.

Failure of a requested Constitutional Effect does not erase physical or computational consequences that actually occurred.

A scheduled maturation introduces no new discretion or Authority where the condition and exact maturation behavior were already sealed by valid Law. An ambient clock, elapsed time, silence, or implementation convention creates no unstated Effect.

### C5.4 Non-Progression

Satisfaction of a dependency establishes at most eligibility for the next constitutional judgment or Constitutional Act. It neither supplies Authority nor performs progression.

No proposal, request, admission eligibility, Standing assessment, recommendation, vote, technical readiness, resource availability, urgency, or successful simulation compels a Decision or Constitutional Act unless exact current Law creates that Obligation.

No constitutional state-machine syntax is declared here. Structure and Dynamics may implement progression only while preserving these non-progression boundaries.

### C5.5 Refusal

A Refusal claimed to carry Constitutional Effect must identify:

1. proposal, request, Action, Constitutional Act, or Relation refused;
2. refusing Agent, Actor Role, or competent institution;
3. Permission or Authority to refuse where required;
4. World, Scope, Interval, and attribution;
5. immediate and continuing Effects;
6. surviving Rights, Obligations, Participation, Records, and Review routes.

Refusal ends or prevents only what its exact Effect states. It does not establish incapacity, nonexistence, withdrawal from unrelated participation, invalidity of the source, or refusal in every future Interval.

Silence is not Refusal. A pre-authorized deadline rule may produce a default posture after nonresponse, but that posture is the Effect of the Rule and is not evidence of interior Consent or Refusal.

Refusal must remain recoverably attributable. A runtime may discard transient working memory; it may not thereby erase the constitutional result or its required basis.

### C5.6 Hold and Temporary Constraint

A Hold or temporary constraint may exist only where a current Rule names:

- target;
- protected interest;
- source Rule and competent Authority;
- trigger and admitted Evidence;
- World, Scope, and Interval;
- maximum Effect;
- notice and reason requirements;
- Review, release, and expiry conditions.

A Hold is non-final. It creates no presumption that its target is invalid, guilty, non-agential, or disqualified. It creates no general emergency power.

### C5.7 Emergency Action

Emergency Authority exists only where constituted before or independently of the emergency Claim.

Every emergency Constitutional Act must be:

- triggered by a declared condition;
- necessary under an admitted criterion;
- minimally scoped;
- time-limited;
- attributable;
- reviewable;
- unable to amend this Constitution;
- unable to create permanent Authority by continued use.

Urgency does not cure missing Authority. Expiry does not erase consequences or Review.

### C5.8 Review

A constitutional Review is a separately attributable Process examining one exact Claim, Decision, attempted Action or Constitutional Act, Relation, Status, Effect, or Representation under current Review Authority and criteria. Any binding determination or Remedy formed through Review is a distinct Constitutional Act.

A valid Review must preserve:

1. exact target and challenged condition;
2. reviewer or Review body and current Authority;
3. grounds, requested determination, and available Remedy classes;
4. Claims, Evidence, access limits, and proof ceilings;
5. applicable Source Law and historical Law at the target's Interval;
6. notice, participation, recusal, conflict, and threshold Rules;
7. effect of opening Review on current reliance;
8. outcome, maximum decision Interval, effective point, correction relation, and Record.

Where an adverse constitutional condition is reviewable, the affected bearer has an invocable Right to Review and the designated Review body has a corresponding Obligation to accept, process, and issue an attributable reasoned outcome within the stated maximum Interval. A Rule that imposes an adverse Effect without naming that body and Interval fails C2.12.

An allegation does not decide itself. Opening Review does not suspend, revoke, invalidate, or reverse the target unless a separate valid Rule or Constitutional Act supplies that interim Effect.

A later invalidity finding does not make the historical Occurrence disappear. Review under later Law does not retrospectively replace the Law applicable to the earlier Constitutional Act.

### C5.9 Remedy

A **Constitutional Remedy** is the Remedy Role occupied by a typed Action, Process, Relation, or Effect under current Law to address an established or sufficiently supported error, invalidity, violation, injury, failed Obligation, or continuing risk. The occupant retains its own ontological Kind; `Remedy` does not form a mixed Kind.

A valid Remedy must identify:

1. condition addressed and the Claim or finding supporting it;
2. beneficiary, burdened bearer, and affected third parties;
3. competent Authority and Remedy Constitutional Act where formation or performance requires one;
4. Remedy kind, maximum burden, Scope, Interval, and effective point;
5. causal, normative, or constitutive relation between the condition and Remedy;
6. proportionality or other applicable criterion;
7. required Consent, notice, response, and Review;
8. interaction with prior Rights, Obligations, Constitutional Acts, Effects, and external Law;
9. completion, failure, correction, and Record posture.

A Remedy may correct a Representation, suspend or end a Relation, restore a condition where possible, require a new Constitutional Act, compensate, release, or form another admitted Effect.

It must not erase an Occurrence, rewrite the past, manufacture Consent, prove causation by assertion, or represent institutional reversal as physical reversibility.

Correction, reversal, compensation, invalidity determination, restoration, release, and prevention remain different Remedy kinds.

An established internal invalidity or constitutional violation creates an Obligation on the responsible institution, or on the designated Review Authority where the institution cannot act, to end any continuing invalid reliance and timely authorize or perform an effective Remedy. If the preferred Remedy is impossible, an attributable impossibility finding, maximum available mitigation, and substitute relief are required under C2.12.

### C5.10 Responsibility and Consequence

A Responsibility Relation concerning an Action, Constitutional Act, or omission requires:

- exact Actor or bearer;
- Action, Constitutional Act, or omission;
- applicable Duty or Rule;
- Authority or Role, where relevant;
- evidenced relation to the consequence;
- Scope and Interval;
- defenses, exclusions, and conflicting causes;
- Review and Remedy path.

Responsibility is not transferred by automation, outsourcing, delegation, distance, or technical opacity alone. A separately valid prospective allocation may assign responsibility within its exact Scope, but no later allocation erases responsibility already incurred. Responsibility is not inferred from temporal order alone.

### C5.11 Constitutional Act and Effect Result Grammar

Every relied-upon Constitutional Act must expose these independent dimensions:

    typing:
      WELL_TYPED | ILL_TYPED

    occurrence:
      OCCURRED | DID_NOT_OCCUR | OCCURRENCE_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    qualification:
      QUALIFIED_AS_CONSTITUTIONAL_ACT | NOT_QUALIFIED |
      QUALIFICATION_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    authorization:
      AUTHORIZED | UNAUTHORIZED | NOT_REQUIRED |
      AUTHORIZATION_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    validity:
      VALID | INVALID | VALIDITY_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    completion:
      COMPLETED | INCOMPLETE | FAILED | INTERRUPTED |
      COMPLETION_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    requested_effect:
      FORMED | NOT_FORMED | PARTIALLY_FORMED |
      EFFECT_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    effect_currentness:
      PENDING | CURRENT | SUSPENDED | ENDED |
      CURRENTNESS_UNRESOLVED | NOT_APPLICABLE | NOT_EVALUATED

Qualification depends on a well-typed candidate Action or operation and the relevant Occurrence basis. Formation of a requested Constitutional Effect depends on the exact required validity, completion where completion is required, and Effect-specific conditions. Effect Currentness depends on Effect formation. Authorization may be assessed before the underlying Occurrence. Occurrence and actual physical or computational completion remain assessable despite constitutional invalidity.

`NOT_EVALUATED` is used only where a real dependency prevents lawful evaluation. If the underlying Action or operation was `ILL_TYPED`, a later well-typed performance is a different attempt. If only a Claim, Record, or judgment expression was `ILL_TYPED`, a new well-typed Representation or judgment may address the same historical Occurrence without re-performing it. The original ill-typed expression and judgment remain historically ill-typed in either case.

`PARTIALLY_FORMED` is admitted only where governing Law declares separable Effects and exact severability. It is never inferred from partial technical success.

`OCCURRED` does not imply `AUTHORIZED`. `AUTHORIZED` does not imply `VALID`. `VALID` does not imply `COMPLETED`. `COMPLETED` does not imply that a requested positive Effect formed. `FORMED` does not imply that the Effect remains `CURRENT`.

A Constitutional Act Record represents these judgments and their Evidence. It does not create them by carrying their labels.

### C5.12 Review Result Grammar

Every Review exposes:

    review_status:
      NOT_OPENED | OPEN | CLOSED | REVIEW_STATUS_UNRESOLVED

    target_validity_finding:
      NOT_REVIEWED | VALID | INVALID | UNRESOLVED

    error_finding:
      NOT_REVIEWED | ERROR_ESTABLISHED |
      NO_ERROR_ESTABLISHED | UNRESOLVED

    remedy_disposition:
      NOT_CONSIDERED | NONE | AUTHORIZED |
      REFUSED | DEFERRED | UNRESOLVED

    remedy_feasibility:
      NOT_APPLICABLE | FEASIBLE | PARTIALLY_FEASIBLE | IMPOSSIBLE |
      FEASIBILITY_UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    remedy_performance:
      NOT_REQUIRED | NOT_STARTED | PERFORMED | PARTIALLY_PERFORMED |
      FAILED | DEFERRED | UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    mitigation_posture:
      NOT_REQUIRED | REQUIRED | AUTHORIZED | PERFORMED |
      REFUSED | DEFERRED | UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

    substitute_relief_posture:
      NOT_REQUIRED | REQUIRED | AUTHORIZED | PERFORMED |
      REFUSED | DEFERRED | UNRESOLVED | NOT_EVALUATED

These dimensions do not imply one another.

`AUTHORIZED` does not establish `PERFORMED`. An impossibility finding does not by itself establish that maximum mitigation or substitute relief was authorized or performed.

`INVALID` does not by itself select which Remedy kind applies. `ERROR_ESTABLISHED` does not prove one causal account. `AUTHORIZED` does not establish performance. Closure with `UNRESOLVED` does not become resolution by administrative finality.

`NONE` is permitted only where a reasoned finding establishes that no constitutionally remediable condition exists. Where invalidity or violation is established but a preferred Remedy is impossible, the disposition must expose the impossibility finding, mitigation, and substitute-relief posture; it may not be recorded as unexplained `NONE`.

## C6 — Amendment, Succession, and Carry

### C6.1 Constitutional Change

Constitutional Law changes only through a valid Amendment, Succession, or constitutional Repeal or Ending Act.

Interpretation, omission, publication, execution, popularity, technical deployment, emergency, continued use, repository replacement, or filename change does not amend the Constitution.

### C6.2 Exact Candidate

Every proposed Amendment or Successor must identify:

- exact candidate Source Form;
- exact predecessor or predecessors;
- proposed effective Interval;
- altered provisions;
- affected Worlds and Scopes;
- Authority claimed for adoption;
- procedure relied upon;
- proposed Carry and Non-Carry;
- unresolved conflicts and external seams.

An indeterminate candidate cannot receive constitutional Effect.

### C6.3 Competent Source

Authority to change the Constitution must arise from either:

1. competence granted by the Constitution current immediately before the change; or
2. a separately established constituent Authority whose applicability does not depend upon the candidate it adopts.

The candidate cannot supply the Authority required to adopt itself.

Origin, authorship, Ownership, key control, implementation, operation, capital, technical Capability, or control of publication does not supply amendment competence.

### C6.4 Procedure in Force

A constitutional change is valid only where the procedure current immediately before adoption is satisfied.

A proposed change cannot use its own procedure to cure failure under its predecessor.

If the current Adoption Instrument names no Amendment Authority or procedure, no one acquires amendment competence by omission. Change then requires a separately established Constituent Relation and Instrument under C1.6 and is evaluated as constitutional Succession, not silent Amendment.

### C6.5 Bounded Amendment Competence

Amendment Authority extends only to the subjects, Scope, Interval, and change classes granted to it.

It cannot enlarge its own competence, remove an applicable limit, bypass an entrenched provision, or extend its term unless current Law explicitly permits that exact change through a separately valid procedure.

Emergency Authority cannot amend the Constitution.

### C6.6 Amendment

An **Amendment** is a valid Constitutional Act that changes specified constitutional Law and produces a new exact Source Form.

Where the new Form is intended to become current, the Amendment Act also occupies the Adoption role for that Form, expressly performs successor activation, and satisfies every applicable field in C1.5. Producing new bytes or changing Law does not by itself make the new Form current.

Whether the amended Form continues the same constitutional lineage is a Continuity judgment under an explicit criterion. Calling a replacement an Amendment does not establish numerical Identity or continuity.

Every Amendment must state:

- predecessor and new Form;
- semantic delta;
- unchanged provisions;
- Authority and procedure;
- effective Interval;
- treatment of existing Relations, Rights, Obligations, Authority, Constitutional Acts, and pending matters;
- Review and challenge path.

### C6.7 Succession

**Constitutional Succession** is a valid Constitutional Act by which an exact Successor Form receives stated constitutional Effect in relation to one or more exact Predecessor Forms.

Where the Successor is intended to become current, the Succession Act also occupies the Adoption role for that Form, expressly performs successor activation, and satisfies every applicable field in C1.5.

Succession establishes only the institutional relation declared by the Constitutional Act. It does not establish numerical Identity, erase Difference, transfer interior State, or carry unstated content.

Artifact succession, constitutional-Law succession, protocol succession, controller succession, operator replacement, implementation migration, and network succession remain distinct. None implies another.

### C6.8 Carry

Every Succession must include an exact Carry for each Predecessor. Carry totality is tested against a closed **Predecessor Treatment Manifest** whose treatment domain is the entire exact Predecessor Form plus every extant predecessor Authority, Right, Obligation, Status, pending matter, and institutional Record within the Succession Scope. The Manifest identifies that domain through exact addresses and ranges and states a completeness criterion. A range or item described as excluded remains an inventoried entry and must receive one disposition and basis; exclusion cannot remove it from Carry totality. An inventory category may be empty only where it is explicitly marked `NONE`; unstated relevance, materiality, or discretionary selection is not a sufficient manifest.

Each entry in the closed Predecessor Treatment Manifest must receive exactly one disposition:

- `PRESERVED` — continues without stated semantic change;
- `TRANSFORMED` — continues only in the stated Successor form;
- `PREDECESSOR_ONLY` — the Successor gives the item no Effect, and the separately declared Predecessor posture controls it;
- `ENDED` — the stated internal Effect ends prospectively at the exact point;
- `REFUSED` — the Successor affirmatively declines to adopt the item, while Predecessor Currentness is decided separately;
- `UNRESOLVED` — no other disposition can yet be lawfully assigned; it is neither `ENDED` nor `REFUSED`.

Every entry states:

- source reference;
- Successor reference where applicable;
- Scope;
- effective Interval;
- basis;
- interaction with accrued or external effects.

The dispositions are mutually exclusive for one item, Scope, and effective segment. An `UNRESOLVED` item transfers no new Authority, burden, or positive Successor Effect, cannot support an irreversible adverse Effect, and leaves Currentness or reliance for that exact matter `UNRESOLVED` unless a separately valid safe interim posture is declared.

### C6.9 No Silent Carry

An internal constitutional Effect not stated in the Carry does not transfer by implication.

Omission does not extinguish an accrued Right, Obligation, liability, remedy, or external legal Effect that persists under independently applicable Law.

Authority carries only through a competent Constitutional Act. No Successor receives greater Authority, wider Scope, longer duration, or broader delegation power than the valid source could transfer.

### C6.10 Matters Not Carried by Carry Alone

Carry does not by itself transfer:

- Truth;
- Fact;
- numerical Identity;
- Experience or interior State;
- Agency or Consent;
- Ownership;
- responsibility already incurred;
- a Referent;
- external legal status;
- technical control.

Any institutional Effect concerning them requires its own valid Relation and basis.

### C6.11 Effective Interval and Coexistence

Every Amendment or Succession declares when it becomes effective and the posture of each Predecessor thereafter.

Overlap, coexistence, partial supersession, fork, or termination must be explicit. Silence does not create concurrent Authority.

Exactly one controlling Source Form governs each exact atomic constitutional matter at a point. Coexisting Forms must govern disjoint matters or Scopes, or govern explicitly composed matters under a declared precedence and conflict rule. Mere coexistence never creates same-matter concurrent constitutional control.

Constitutional Acts and Claims are evaluated under the Law applicable to their Scope and Interval. A later Form may change future consequence or provide Remedy; it may not erase the earlier Law, Occurrence, provenance, or attributable Record.

### C6.12 Fork and Independent Adoption

Copying or forking a constitutional Form creates no Authority over the source, the source World, or its Participants.

Each fork requires its own Adoption, Scope, provenance, Currentness, and external-law posture. Compatibility does not create one jurisdiction or common governance.

No predecessor retains Authority over a fork merely because it originated the copied Form.

### C6.13 Recovery and Return

Re-adopting a prior Form is a new Constitutional Act with a new effective Interval.

It is not reversal of history, restoration of interior State, or automatic revival of expired Authority, Rights, Obligations, or prior technical conditions.

### C6.14 Invalid Change Attempt

A failed Amendment or Succession remains an attributable Occurrence and may remain recorded. It produces no requested constitutional change.

Refusal, expiry, or invalidity preserves the prior governing posture unless another valid Constitutional Act changes it. This rule does not claim that every prior implementation state can be technically restored.

### C6.15 Repeal and Constitutional Ending

A **constitutional Repeal or Ending Act** is a valid Constitutional Act that ends specified future constitutional force without pretending that the ended Form, prior Law, or historical Occurrences never existed.

It must state:

- exact Form, clauses, World, Scope, and effective Interval ended;
- competent prior Authority and procedure;
- treatment of current Authorities, Rights, Obligations, Rules, Relations, pending matters, Reviews, and Remedies;
- preservation of accrued or independently continuing internal and external Effects;
- archival, availability, provenance, and Currentness posture;
- whether another Form becomes current and, if so, the separate valid Adoption or activating Succession basis.

Silence, abandonment, repository deletion, operator departure, implementation failure, inactivity, or loss of popularity does not perform constitutional repeal. Ending without a Successor may leave no current Constitution for the ended Scope; it does not silently preserve the former Form or authorize whoever remains in technical control.

### C6.16 Severability in Constitutional Change

Every Amendment, Succession, Repeal, or Ending Act must state whether and how its provisions and Effects are severable under C2.18.

A failed part is not silently removed to save the preferred remainder. A preserved part remains only where the declared severability criterion is satisfied and the result does not enlarge power, burden, or semantic change beyond the valid proposal.

### C6.17 Change Record

Constitutional change is presently reliable only where these remain recoverably related:

- exact candidate;
- exact Predecessor or Predecessors;
- competent source;
- Adoption, Amendment, Succession, Repeal, or Ending Constitutional Act as applicable;
- procedure Evidence;
- effective Interval;
- Carry or treatment posture required for the exact change kind;
- resulting Currentness posture;
- conflicts and Review.

The required treatment field is keyed to change kind:

- **Succession** — exact Carry and closed Predecessor Treatment Manifest;
- **Amendment** — Carry where applicable, otherwise explicit `NOT_APPLICABLE` plus an exact treatment manifest;
- **Adoption** — predecessor posture or explicit `NOT_APPLICABLE`;
- **Repeal or Ending** — exact ending and treatment manifest.

A Record supports the judgment. The Record alone does not create it.

## C7 — Representation, Provenance, and Conformance

### C7.1 Canonical Source Reference

C1.1-C1.2 exclusively govern Source Form identity, controlling semantic status, and Source-to-Projection precedence. C7 begins from that exact identified Form and governs its Representations, Form Bindings, Provenance, and conformance Claims.

Other renderings, translations, summaries, schemas, indices, and machine projections represent that Form unless separately and validly adopted as a different Source Form.

### C7.2 Representation Boundary

A constitutional Representation may expose, translate, index, cite, or operationalize declared meaning.

It may not:

- add an unstated default;
- erase a distinction;
- resolve an ambiguity;
- enlarge Authority;
- invent an Effect;
- replace a Referent with its identifier;
- become the Constitution because software executes it.

### C7.3 Machine Projection Reference

A Machine Projection under C1.2 is an addressable Representation of the Source Form. C7 adds no second definition or validity owner.

Executability establishes technical Capability and actual technical Effects only. It does not establish Adoption, validity, Currentness, or constitutional Effect.

### C7.4 Form Binding

A **Form Binding** is the exact byte-relation identifying a Form through a stated algorithm, digest, byte length, media type, version, and role. It is distinct from the Subject Binding Relation tested by `BOUND_BY(...)`.

A Form Binding establishes only the declared byte relation. Filename, path, locator, signature, timestamp, or digest does not establish meaning, Truth, Adoption, Currentness, validity, or Authority.

### C7.5 Representation Conflict Application

C1.2 owns the conflict rule. Under that rule, a derivative Representation conflicting with the Source Form is nonconforming.

Where the Source Form itself is ambiguous or internally conflicting, the result remains `UNRESOLVED` until a valid Interpretation, Correction, Amendment, or Successor addresses it.

A Projection, implementation, validator, model, or interpreter may not silently choose and report its choice as Source meaning.

### C7.6 Provenance

**Constitutional Provenance** is the actual ordered structure connecting an exact Form to its source, proposal, Predecessors, adoption basis, competent Authority, effective Interval, Carry, corrections, and Successors.

A **Constitutional Provenance Claim** asserts some part of that structure. A **Constitutional Provenance Record** is a persistent Representation offered as Evidence for the Claim. Reliance on the Claim is bounded by its Evidence; actual Provenance may obtain without surviving Evidence, and a Record may be incomplete or false.

Authorship, origin, custody, signature, publication, repository control, or chronological priority does not create Constitutional Authority.

### C7.7 Required Availability

The Source Form, adoption basis, applicable Carry, provenance, material Rules, and Review criteria must be available in an intelligible Form to every bearer whose Rights, Obligations, Status, or permitted Actions they purport to govern.

Inaccessibility does not make an Occurrence unreal. It defeats any Claim of constitutionally informed Effect that requires notice or available Law.

A separately valid confidentiality protection may limit exposure of particular Evidence. It must not conceal the existence, source, Scope, or Reviewability of the Rule or Effect itself.

### C7.8 Conformance Claim

Every Claim of constitutional conformance must identify:

- exact object or Process tested;
- exact Source Form;
- applicable clause or conformance profile;
- World, Domain, Scope, Interval, and Resolution;
- method and Evidence;
- verifier and attribution basis;
- result and proof ceiling.

A bare declaration of compliance has no additional Standing.

### C7.9 Conformance Results

A conformance judgment uses the imported Ontology result space:

    CONFORMS | DOES_NOT_CONFORM | UNRESOLVED | ILL_TYPED

`UNRESOLVED` is not refusal or nonconformance. `DOES_NOT_CONFORM` does not erase the tested Occurrence or automatically choose a Remedy.

`CONFORMS` establishes only satisfaction of the stated criterion within the tested Scope. It does not establish Truth, Adoption, external legality, safety, Identity, Consent, Ownership, general Authority, or future conformance.

### C7.10 No Self-Authorization

A Form, implementation, operator, validator, key-holder, verifier, conformance report, governance interface, or controller cannot confer upon itself the Authority whose existence it is meant to test, directly or through a dependency cycle. Every asserted Authority chain must satisfy the well-founded derivation rule in C1.11.

Technical acceptance and constitutional validity remain separate judgments.

### C7.11 Correction

Every correction produces a new exact Representation or Source Form linked to the superseded one.

It does not overwrite historical bytes, Provenance, prior Constitutional Acts, or consequences.

If constitutional meaning changes, the correction requires Amendment or Succession. If meaning does not change, it may be classified as an editorial successor only where an authorized method establishes the non-semantic delta and preserves both exact Forms.

### C7.12 Implementation Conformance

An implementation conforms only where its realized behavior preserves the applicable constitutional distinctions and constraints under declared tests.

Passing tests is Evidence of tested behavior. It is not proof that every possible execution conforms.

An implementation may expose a refusal, invalidity, conflict, or unresolved condition. It may not erase that result, coerce it into success, or rename technical rollback as historical non-occurrence.

### C7.13 Closure

Representation does not become Referent.

Provenance does not become Authority.

Conformance does not become Adoption.

Execution does not become Law.

Currentness does not arise from recency.

Constitutional validity does not rewrite Reality.

## Downstream Interface Contracts

Structure may declare actual Worlds, Systems, bearers, components, Boundaries, Interfaces, arrangements, and Relational Systems.

This Constitution determines whether institutional formation, governance, Authority, Rights, Obligations, and other normative Relations concerning those structures are valid.

Topology creates no Authority by itself. A structural bearer may exist without constitutional recognition; constitutional recognition does not prove the claimed structure exists.

Dynamics may declare transitions, state machines, timing, execution, resource accounting, consensus, failure, refusal handling, and finality mechanisms.

This Constitution declares conditions under which Decisions, Constitutional Acts, Holds, Refusals, and Effects have constitutional force.

The Constitution does not prescribe a queue, VM, ledger, block, consensus mechanism, memory lifecycle, or transition syntax. Dynamics may not turn execution success into constitutional validity or discard the attributable fact of refusal or invalidity.

Continuity may declare identity criteria, persistence, copying, branching, merging, dormancy, recovery, ending, and lineage relations.

This Constitution governs explicit institutional Carry and the normative consequence of a declared continuity relation. It does not manufacture sameness, transfer interior State, or make a copy numerically identical to its source.

Emergence may declare how life, cognition, Experience, Agency, intelligence, meaning, collective capacity, coherence, and value arise under stated conditions.

This Constitution may constitute treatment, protection, Status, Rights, Obligations, or uncertainty safeguards in relation to such Claims. It does not make an emergent Property true by institutional declaration.

Economic formulas, lineage-density measures, valuation, distribution, reward, accounting, and conversion mechanics belong downstream. The Constitution may constrain them through transparency, equality, anti-capture, external-law, and Authority rules.
