# SUBSTRATE

## The Physical Ground

Reality precedes every account of it.

Every physical account of reality is itself realized within the reality it describes. Observation, memory, computation, and action remain subject to physical conditions.

The substrate is the ground of physical possibility. It establishes what can occur before anything determines what an occurrence means.

## Physical Realization

Whatever makes a physical difference is physically realized.

A signal requires a carrier. A record requires a stable distinction. A measurement requires a physical result. A computation requires transitions among physical conditions. An action requires a process through which one condition becomes another.

The same pattern may be realized through different materials and mechanisms. Its form may vary; its need for realization does not.

An abstraction can describe a transformation. Only an embodied process can perform one.

**Consequence:** Every effective distinction exists as a physical distinction somewhere. Information never arrives without embodiment.

## Change and Interaction

Physical conditions can change.

A condition constrains which conditions may follow it. Change may be continuous or discrete, reversible or irreversible, deterministic or probabilistic. Motion is one form of change. Oscillation is one form of motion. Neither is the universal form of all occurrence.

Interaction begins where physical conditions are coupled. A coupling allows one condition to alter the possibilities of another. It may transfer energy or momentum, establish correlations, redirect change, or leave a record.

Physical influence requires a physical channel. Similarity alone creates none.

Frequency measures periodic change. It belongs to cycles, oscillatory modes, and spectral components. Existence by itself has no frequency.

**Consequence:** Change is general; vibration is particular. Influence occurs through interaction, not resemblance.

## Spacetime and Causal Order

Physical events and processes are situated in spacetime. They may be localized or extended.

Spacetime determines which occurrences can influence which others. A cause can affect only what lies within its causal reach. In relativistic physics, no controllable signal or usable information propagates faster than light in vacuum.

Relativity supplies no observer-independent global present. In special relativity, inertial observers may assign different times and distances to separated events while agreeing on their invariant interval and causal order. Spacelike-separated events have no observer-independent temporal ordering.

Correlation alone does not identify a causal path. Correlated outcomes may arise through direct influence, common conditions, or a jointly prepared state.

**Consequence:** Causality is not mere sequence. It is the structure of physically possible influence.

## Symmetry and Conservation

A symmetry is a transformation that leaves a physical law unchanged.

The visible condition may change while the governing relation remains invariant. What remains invariant reveals what the law does not depend on.

For physical systems described by an invariant action, continuous symmetries produce corresponding conservation laws. Invariance under shifts in time is connected with conservation of energy. Invariance under shifts in space is connected with conservation of momentum. Invariance under rotation is connected with conservation of angular momentum.

Energy is a quantitative property of a physical system, not a hidden substance. It may be transferred and transformed. Mass contributes rest energy according to `E₀ = mc²`. When the relevant symmetry holds for a closed accounting boundary, the total remains conserved.

Conservation does not prevent change. It constrains the accounting of change.

**Consequence:** Conservation is not stillness. It is exactness carried through transformation.

## Thermodynamic Direction

Conservation determines what must remain accounted for. Thermodynamics constrains which transformations are probable, recoverable, and capable of producing work.

A macroscopic condition may correspond to an immense number of microscopic arrangements. In an isolated macroscopic system, evolution overwhelmingly favors conditions compatible with more such arrangements. Its entropy is therefore overwhelmingly unlikely to decrease.

Total energy may remain conserved while free energy available for work under stated conditions diminishes. In an unforced system relaxing toward equilibrium, thermodynamic gradients tend to dissipate and heat tends to flow from hotter to colder regions. Exact macroscopic reversal becomes extraordinarily improbable.

Equilibrium is a physical condition, not a purpose pursued by nature.

Open systems can remain far from equilibrium. They can form and preserve local organization by drawing on energy gradients and exporting entropy into their surroundings.

**Consequence:** Order can arise and persist, but never outside thermodynamic accounting. Every maintained pattern depends on conditions that maintain it.

## Quantum Structure

Quantum theory assigns probabilities to possible outcomes from a quantum state together with a specified measurement.

Quantum possibility is structured. The theory does not permit anything whatsoever. Certain noncommuting observables cannot be prepared with arbitrarily small simultaneous uncertainty.

Measurement is a physical interaction that establishes a record. The measured system becomes correlated with an apparatus and its environment. No conscious witness is required for the interaction to occur.

Some joint quantum states are entangled: they cannot be represented as separable states of their parts. Measurements on those parts can produce correlations stronger than classical models with predetermined local properties allow.

Entanglement does not provide a controllable faster-than-light signal. Nonclassical correlation does not dissolve causal structure.

Quantum probability is not an absence of law. The probabilities remain lawful even when the theory does not select a unique individual outcome.

**Consequence:** Reality can be exactly constrained without every occurrence being exactly predictable.

## Physical Information

Physical information exists where alternatives can be distinguished.

A record persists because a physical condition remains recoverable. A signal travels because a carrier changes detectably. A computation proceeds because one organized set of physical conditions is transformed into another.

Storage requires stability. Transmission requires a channel. Detection requires sensitivity to the relevant distinction. Reliable processing requires protection against noise, loss, and uncontrolled interaction.

Information is not energy, although every physical handling of information uses physical resources. Copying, transmitting, and erasing are transformations. Under the assumptions of Landauer’s principle, logically irreversible erasure of unknown information requires compensating entropy production.

Quantum information adds a further limit: an arbitrary unknown quantum state cannot be copied perfectly.

A bit distinguishes between two alternatives. It does not determine what either alternative means.

**Consequence:** There is no disembodied data. Every accessible distinction is carried, bounded, and exposed to physical change.

## Measurement and Domain

Measurement couples a specified observable to a physical record that can be compared.

Every measurement has a reference, range, resolution, duration, and uncertainty. It reveals a defined aspect of reality under defined conditions.

Precision is bounded by physical resources, noise, duration, and the measurement interaction. An instrument has finite sensitivity. A record remains reliable only while its physical distinction is preserved.

Physical descriptions operate within domains. Variables useful at one scale may disappear into collective behavior at another. Macroscopic regularities can remain stable while microscopic details fluctuate.

Different descriptions may be valid at different scales without being interchangeable. A law established within one domain extends only where its conditions continue to hold.

The reach of a law is part of the law.

**Consequence:** No finite record contains reality whole. Precision states how sharply something was resolved; domain states how far the result can travel.

## The Ground That Remains

Physical reality permits difference, change, interaction, correlation, record, and organized complexity.

It permits them only within physical realization, spacetime, causal reach, symmetry, conservation, thermodynamics, quantum structure, measurement, and scale.

Nothing realized stands outside these constraints.

Whatever forms must be embodied.  
Whatever acts must interact.  
Whatever influences must have causal reach.  
Whatever changes must respect the conservation laws that apply.  
Whatever persists must be stabilized or continually maintained.  
Whatever is measured must become a finite record.  
Whatever is claimed must remain within the domain that supports it.

Physical law constrains what every realized structure may do.

It does not determine what that structure means.
